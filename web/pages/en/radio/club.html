The Millbrook Amateur Radio Society

The Society was founded in 1971 by seven men and a borrowed receiver. Today we have 43 members, from a boy of fourteen to a lady of eighty-one who still sends Morse faster than any of us. We meet on the first Thursday of every month at 8:00 PM, in the back room of the fire station, and the coffee is made by the youngest member, who is not allowed to forget the sugar.

What the club does
  • Runs the 2 metre repeater above the water tower, which covers the whole lake
  • Teaches the licence course twice a year, with a pass rate of nine out of ten
  • Provides emergency communications: in the flood of 1991 the Society kept the town hall and the hospital in touch for four days
  • Visits schools and shows pupils how a radio contact happens, with a Morse key and a pencil
  • Keeps the library: forty-two books, two cabinets of components, and a soldering iron that nobody will admit to owning

Join us. The fee is $12 a year, $6 for students and for retired members who ask politely. There is no test to join, only a handshake. Come to a meeting, sit at the back, and tell the person next to you what you want to learn.

Contacts: the secretary is Mrs. Wheeler, who answers letters sent to the Society, care of the fire station, Millbrook. The president is the one with the beard. There are two with beards; the president is the taller.
