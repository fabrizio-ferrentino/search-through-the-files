CQ CQ CQ - this is W3MBK

Welcome to my shack, on the air from Millbrook since 1983. My name is Harvey, I am forty-eight, and I work in the day for the water board. In the evening I come down to the spare room under the roof, switch on the radio and listen to the world. This page is my logbook, my notebook and my way of saying thank you to the people who answered when I called.

What is amateur radio? It is a hobby, and a service: anyone who passes the examination can transmit on bands set aside by the government for the purpose, talk to other amateurs in the next street or on the other side of the world, build their own equipment, and help when the telephones are down. We do not make money from it, and we are not allowed to. We make friends, and now and then a very good antenna.
