The interview

Before. Find out what the company does. Prepare three things you want them to know about you. Choose your clothes the evening before. Arrive ten minutes early, not thirty.

During. Shake hands, look people in the eye, listen to the question before answering. It is normal to be nervous; it is fine to say so. If you do not know something, say "I do not know, but I can learn".

The questions they always ask: "Tell me about yourself" (two minutes, about work, not your whole life); "Why do you want this job?"; "What are your weaknesses?" (a real one, and what you do about it).

After. If they do not call you back within two weeks, phone them politely. A "no" is better than silence, and sometimes the phone call changes their mind.

One more thing. An interview is not an exam of who you are as a person. If it goes badly, it went badly that day, with those people, for that job. Nothing more.
