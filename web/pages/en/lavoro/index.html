Looking for a job?

JobFinder collects the offers of the companies of the region, gives practical advice on CVs and interviews, and has a forum where people looking for work help each other. It is free, for those looking and for those offering.

The figures of the month: 312 offers online · 1,840 registered CVs · the most requested jobs: sales assistants, warehouse staff, programmers, nurses.

Sending your CV online is suspended while we renew the database. Answer the offers directly, by post or by phone, using the contacts in each offer.

New on JobFinder: the forum, where you can ask questions, share your experience and get advice from people who have been through it. Read the rules before you write.
