Forum rules

  1. This forum is for people looking for work. Be useful: share what you know.
  2. No insults. Looking for work is hard enough without being mocked.
  3. No advertising of products, courses or "methods". Posts that advertise are removed.
  4. Do not publish your phone number or address. Companies contact you through their offers.
  5. Write clearly, with a title that says what you are asking.

Writing in the forum is suspended while we renew the database, like the CV service. The topics can be read.

The moderators are volunteers who found work through JobFinder and wanted to give something back.
