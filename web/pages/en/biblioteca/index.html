Welcome to the library

Twenty-eight thousand books, two hundred videotapes, the local newspapers since 1952 and, since last autumn, two computers connected to the Internet. Membership is free for everyone who lives or works in Millbrook and the villages around it.

> Story hour for children every Saturday at 10:30.
> Closed on Easter Monday.
> New: the shelf of books in large print, near the entrance.

The library is a quiet place, but not a silent one. You can talk at a low voice, you can study in groups in the back room, and you can ask the librarians anything: if they do not know the answer, they know which book has it.

INTERNET
CORNER		Our two computers can be booked at the desk for one hour at a time. Children under fourteen must be accompanied by an adult. Please do not change the settings: last week someone set the screen to green letters on black, and a pensioner thought the computer had broken.
