We are copying the paper card catalogue into the computer, one card at a time. So far we have reached the letter F. When the work is finished you will be able to search the catalogue from home. Until then, the card catalogue in the entrance hall is at your disposal, and so are the librarians.

Search form (the catalogue terminal in the hall will use the same one):
Title:
Author:
Section:
Look for: books magazines both
 

How to use the card catalogue. The drawers in the hall hold one card for each book. The cards are filed three times: by author, by title and by subject. Each card carries a code, written in pencil in the top corner: that code tells you on which shelf the book lives.

823 HARHARDING, Elsa.
The Lighthouse Keeper's Winter. 1998.
Subjects: lighthouses; winter; fiction.
(a card as you will find it in the drawer)

The shelves follow the old decimal system. Novels have their own room, in alphabetical order by author.

The local section is in the back room, behind the glass door: books about Millbrook and the valley, the town council's reports, the parish bulletins and the local newspaper, bound by year since 1952. The most recent years are on microfilm: ask at the desk for the reader.

A volunteer asked us to add a note here: "The letter F took four months. Please be patient with us until Z".
