Thursday evening the reading room stays open until ten for students and for anyone who needs a quiet, warm place to read. The loan desk closes at seven as usual.

Summer hours (July and August): mornings only, from 9 AM to 1 PM, Monday to Saturday. In the second and third week of August the library is closed for cleaning and inventory.

Holidays. The library is closed on public holidays and on the feast of the town's patron saint. When a holiday falls on a Tuesday, we also close on the Monday afternoon before it, to give the staff a long weekend. They deserve it.

The book drop. When the library is closed you can return books through the slot in the door. Do not use it for videotapes: they break. Do not use it for letters to the mayor either: that is the next door.
