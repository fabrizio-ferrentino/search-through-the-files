How to borrow

Membership. Bring an identity document to the desk: the card is made on the spot and is free. Children under fourteen need the signature of a parent.

How many and for how long. Up to three books for thirty days, and two videotapes for seven days. You can renew a loan once, by phone or at the desk, if nobody else has reserved the book.

Reservations. If a book is on loan, you can reserve it at the desk: we will call you when it comes back and keep it for you for five days. Reservations through the Internet are not active: the service was planned for this spring but the town council has postponed the money to next year.

Late returns. We do not charge fines. We send a postcard. After the third postcard, the librarian calls you. After the librarian, nobody has ever needed anything else.

Lost or damaged books. It happens. Tell us: we will ask you to replace the book with a new copy or, if it is no longer on sale, with another book chosen together.

Interlibrary loan. If we do not have a book, we can ask for it from another library in the province. It takes about two weeks and costs the price of the stamp.
