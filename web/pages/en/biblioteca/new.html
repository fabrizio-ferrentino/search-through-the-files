New on the shelves - March 1999

823 HARHARDING, Elsa.
The Lighthouse Keeper's Winter. 1998.
A novel.

The book of the month for the reading group. A man, a lighthouse, a winter, and the letters that nobody brings him. Three copies, already all on loan.

004 BENBENNETT, Walter.
Your First Computer. 1998.
A manual.

Clear, patient, with large print. Recommended by the participants of our course for the over-sixties.

635 LOWLOWELL, Anne.
Gardening on a Balcony. 1997.
Practical.

For those who have no garden and want tomatoes anyway.

779 REAREAD, Thomas.
Mountains of the North. 1998.
Photographs.

Large format, for consultation only: it does not leave the reading room.

823 CARCARTER, Joseph.
The Detective Who Never Slept. 1999.
A thriller.

The fourth in the series. The librarian's comment: "He still does not sleep. We still do not either".

398 GRAGRAY, Mabel.
Grandma's Fables. 1997.
Children.

Twenty stories to read aloud before bed. Ideal for story hour.

158 WESWEST, Dorothy.
Learning to Say No. 1998.
Self-help.

Twelve people on the waiting list. We find this interesting.

Would you like the library to buy a book? Write the title on the cards at the desk. We read all of them; we buy what the budget allows, starting with the titles asked for by more than one reader.
