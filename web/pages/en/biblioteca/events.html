Events

EVERY
SATURDAY
10:30 AM		Story hour. For children from four to eight. A volunteer reads, the children listen, the parents drink a coffee in the hall. Everyone is happy.

THU
04/08/1999
9 PM		Reading group. We talk about The Lighthouse Keeper's Winter. New readers welcome; you do not need to have finished the book, but it helps.

TUESDAYS
IN APRIL
3 PM		Computer course for the over-sixties. Four lessons: switching on, the mouse, writing a letter, and the Internet. Ten places, already full. A second course will start in May.

FRI
04/23/1999
6 PM		World Book Day. Every reader who brings a book to swap takes one home. Last year we swapped three hundred.

SAT
05/15/1999
4 PM		Local history afternoon. The old newspapers of Millbrook, the photographs of the 1966 flood, and the memories of those who were there.

All events are free. For the courses, sign up at the desk. For story hour, just come: the children sit on the carpet, the adults on the chairs, and nobody sits on the books.
