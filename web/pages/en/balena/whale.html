the 52 hertz whale
« back to the room

Let me introduce her, since I stole her name.

At the end of the eighties the US Navy had hydrophones, underwater microphones, scattered across the Pacific Ocean to listen for submarines. Instead of submarines, at some point, they heard a whale. A strange whale: it sang at 52 hertz.

Whales of her kind sing much lower, between 15 and 25 hertz. She sings higher than all the others. Scientists followed her for years. Every year she comes back, makes her journey alone, and sings. Her song changes a little with the seasons, like the others' do. Only the others probably cannot hear her. Or they hear her and do not understand. Not one has ever answered.

they call her the loneliest whale in the world.

i don't think she knows.
i think she keeps singing because she thinks that sooner or later someone will answer.

When I read about her, in a newspaper someone left in the warehouse, I cut out the article and stuck it above my monitor. It is still there. It has gone yellow.

Sometimes I think I sing at the wrong frequency too. I say things, but they come out crooked. At work, at weddings, with girls. Especially with girls. I talk and they hear something else, or nothing at all. Maybe it is my fault. Maybe there is a right way to sing and I never learned it.

But she keeps going. Every year. Across the whole ocean. So I keep going too: this page is my song. If you are reading it, in a way, you heard me.

If anyone knows where to find a recording of her song, please write it in the guestbook. I have been looking for a long time.
