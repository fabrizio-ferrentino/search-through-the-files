Rules

  1. Ads are free and are published within a week of arrival.
  2. An ad stays online for six months. It can be renewed by writing to the editors before it expires.
  3. Lonely hearts ads that receive no answer in six months expire automatically. You can send them again: sometimes a new month brings new readers.
  4. We do not publish ads that offer money for meetings, that insult anyone, or that sell "methods" of any kind.
  5. We do not check what is sold. Meet in public places, never pay in advance, and trust your instinct.
  6. Answers to the lonely hearts go through the editors. We never give out anyone's address.

The board is run by three volunteers in their spare time. Be patient with us.
