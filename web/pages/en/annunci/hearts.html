Lonely hearts

People looking for people: friendship, love, company, a partner for dancing or for bridge. To answer an ad, write to the editors quoting its reference: we forward your letter without giving anyone's address. The column's archive, month by month, is here.

Ref. 3205 · 01/1999

A note from the editors: the ads that receive the most answers are not the ones of the most beautiful or the richest. They are the ones in which a person says something true about themselves.
