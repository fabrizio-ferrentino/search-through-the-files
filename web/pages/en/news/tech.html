Millennium bug: nine months to go
03/31/1999 · One company in three has not checked yet.
The Internet café opens in the old cinema
03/29/1999 · Eight computers, one modem.

The family computer gets cheaper. A complete PC with a modem now costs less than a second-hand scooter. Shops say that the most common buyer is a father with two children "who need it for school" - and that most of the time, after a month, the father is the one using it at night.

A virus in the e-mail. A new virus travels as an attachment with a friendly subject line. Opening it sends the same message to everyone in your address book. The advice: do not open attachments you did not expect, even if they come from a friend.

How many are we online? According to the providers' association, the Internet subscriptions in the country have doubled in a year. Most connect in the evening, after eight, when the phone call costs less: that is why the line is always busy at that hour.

Glossary for beginners. Modem: the box that lets the computer use the phone line. Browser: the program you are using to read this page. Home page: the first page of a site. Chat: a written conversation with strangers, in real time. Spam: the mail nobody asked for.
