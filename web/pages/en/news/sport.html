Sport

The Cup goes to the wire
03/29/1999 · A goal in the ninetieth minute.

Cycling. The spring classics open on Saturday with the race along the coast. The favourite has a broken wrist and will start anyway: "I steer with the other hand", he joked at the presentation.

Basketball. The home team loses at the buzzer and drops to fourth place. The coach: "We played for forty minutes and switched off for four seconds". The fans at the exit were less philosophical.

Skiing. The season ends with little snow and a lot of rain. The resorts count the losses; the ski schools hope for Easter, "if it snows on the palms".
