The Cup goes to the wire
A goal in the ninetieth minute keeps the final round open. Next Sunday, all against all.
03/29/1999 · Sport desk

Ninety minutes of rain, a goalkeeper in a state of grace and, in the last minute, a header from the smallest man on the pitch: the Rovers beat City one-nil and the Cup, which seemed decided, goes to the last round.

"I closed my eyes and jumped", the scorer told the reporters, still covered in mud. His coach was less romantic: "We practised that corner for three weeks. Three weeks. Ask the boys".

Next Sunday four teams will play for two places in the final. The tickets for the home match were sold out in an afternoon; the club has promised a big screen in the car park for those who stayed outside.
