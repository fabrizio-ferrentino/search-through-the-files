World

The single currency is three months old. Since January the euro exists on bank statements and in the stock exchanges, but nobody has yet held one in their hand: coins and notes will arrive in 2002. In the meantime the shops print double prices, and calculators with the conversion key are selling out.

The balloon that wants to go round the world. Two pilots took off again from the mountains for the umpteenth attempt to circle the Earth without landing. Their capsule is the size of a lift. "We have food for three weeks and patience for two", one of them told the radio.

Floods in the south of the continent. The rivers burst their banks after ten days of rain: thousands of people have left their homes. International aid is arriving by helicopter, because the roads have gone with the water.

The space station grows. A second module has been joined to the first in orbit. Astronauts will live on board from next year. The cost, says the critics' estimate, is that of a medium-sized country's motorways.

Mobile phones: one in five. For the first time a European country has more mobile phones than fixed lines in homes. Sociologists are divided: freedom, or the end of the family phone in the hall where everybody could hear everything?
