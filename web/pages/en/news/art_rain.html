Late frost, the orchards hold their breath
Two nights below freezing in the plains. Farmers lit fires between the rows to save the blossom.
03/30/1999 · National desk

Minus three degrees at five in the morning, with the peach trees in full blossom: for the farmers of the plains it is the worst possible combination. For two nights, from the main road, you could see small fires burning in the orchards, lit to keep the air a few degrees warmer around the flowers.

"We stayed up all night to keep them going", says a grower. "My father did it, my grandfather did it. You sit in the dark among the trees and you listen for the wind". The damage will only be known in a week, when the flowers that froze turn brown.

The farmers' association asks the region to declare a state of natural disaster in the worst-hit areas. The forecasters, meanwhile, promise a return to milder temperatures from the weekend.
