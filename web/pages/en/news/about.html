About us

NewsToday has been on the Internet since the spring of 1997. We are a small newsroom - six journalists, a photographer and a technician who keeps the server running - and we publish the news of the day every morning at seven, with updates in the afternoon when something important happens.

We are not a newspaper, and we do not want to replace one. We are a quick read for the coffee break: the main facts, written clearly, without shouting. If you want to know more, buy a paper. Really.

Write to us. We read every letter, but we cannot answer all of them. Our address is in the WebNet directory under "newsroom". We do not publish letters without a name, and we do not publish letters that insult anyone.

Corrections. When we make a mistake we say so, here and in the article. The latest: in the article on the frost we wrote "minus thirteen" instead of "minus three". A reader from the plains wrote to us that if it had been minus thirteen he would not have been there to read us.

The archive of articles older than one month is being moved to a new server and is not available at the moment.
