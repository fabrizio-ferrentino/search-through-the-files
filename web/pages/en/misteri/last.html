last update

This site will not be updated any more.

In the last weeks I have received mails at three in the morning. Always at three. No sender, no subject. Small letters, no capitals. They do not ask anything. They tell me things about my house that I have never written here: where my desk is, that the window behind it faces the street, that I live alone.

The last one said: you collected my letters. now you know me. i know you too.

I am sure there is an explanation. A joke, a reader who lives nearby. I am a reasonable man. But I have unplugged the modem, and tonight I will sleep at my sister's, and tomorrow I will take this computer to the tip. Not to sell it. To the tip.

The site stays online until the provider closes it. If you are reading this at night, on your own, my advice is the only one I have ever given: switch off, and go and sleep near someone.
