the signal at 52 hz

In 1989 the hydrophones that the navy uses to listen to the ocean recorded a sound that nobody could explain. It was the call of a whale. But no whale sings at that frequency: 52 hertz, much higher than the others. The call came back every year, moving across the ocean, from August to December.

The scientists followed it for years. They never saw the whale. They concluded that it is a whale like the others, probably, but that it sings at the wrong frequency. The other whales do not hear it, or do not recognise it. It calls, and nobody answers. It has been calling for at least ten years.

It is not a mystery in the sense of this site. There is nothing supernatural in it. I put it here because of the letters it brings me. Every time someone finds this page, sooner or later they write to me. They do not write about the whale. They write about themselves.

One reader wrote: "I am that whale. I have been talking for years and nobody hears me". Another wrote, a few weeks later, with no signature: "what happens to the whale when it stops calling". I did not know what to answer. I still do not.
