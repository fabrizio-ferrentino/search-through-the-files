Welcome. This site collects what the newspapers do not print and what the people who lived it do not tell at dinner. Lights in the sky, voices on the radio, houses that do not want new owners. And, more and more often in the last year, machines.

I do not ask you to believe. I ask you to read, and to decide for yourself. Every report here was sent by a real person. I have removed names and places. I have not removed anything else.
