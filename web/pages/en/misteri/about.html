who I am

A man of fifty-three who reads too much and sleeps too little. For twenty years I worked as an electrician; I have been inside more houses than a postman. You learn things, going into houses. You learn which ones are cold for no reason, which ones the dogs will not enter, which ones the families leave in a hurry.

I started this site in 1996 to collect the stories I had heard. I thought I would get letters about ghosts and flying saucers, and I did. But what people really write to me about is being alone with something they cannot explain, and nobody to tell.

I am not a medium, a priest or a scientist. I do not sell anything. If you need help, real help, I will tell you to talk to a doctor, or to the people you live with. I have lost count of the letters in which the real mystery was that nobody in the house was talking to anybody.
