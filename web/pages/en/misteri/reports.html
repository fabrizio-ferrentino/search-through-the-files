reports: home computers

CASE 41 · 11/1998 · the screen that stays on
"Every morning I find the computer on. I switch it off at night, I pull the plug. In the morning it is on, with the screen showing the desktop, as if someone had just stopped using it. The chair is pulled out. My wife says it is me, sleepwalking. I have never sleepwalked in my life."

CASE 44 · 01/1999 · someone in the reflection
"When the screen is dark you can see the room behind you in it. Last night I saw a small figure standing in the doorway behind my chair. A child, I think. I turned around and there was nobody. We do not have children. I put a blanket over the monitor. I could still feel it looking."

CASE 47 · 02/1999 · the wrong words
"My son writes his homework on the computer. Yesterday I found a page in the middle of his history essay, written in small letters, with no capitals: nobody wanted me so nobody gets to stay. He says he did not write it. I believe him. It is not the way he writes. It is not the way anyone in our house writes."

I asked all three if their computer had been bought new. Two said it was second-hand. The third did not answer my mail.
