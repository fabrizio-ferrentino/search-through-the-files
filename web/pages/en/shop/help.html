Frequently asked questions

Is shopping on the Internet safe?
Yes. If you don't feel like typing in your credit card number, choose cash on delivery: you only pay when you have the parcel in your hands.

I have never bought anything on the Internet. How does it work?
Just like in a shop: you choose, you put it in the cart and you go to the checkout. Instead of the checkout there is a page where you write your address. If you get lost, call the toll-free number: someone will walk you through it step by step.

How long does delivery take?
Usually a week. Around the holidays, especially in December, a few days more: order your Christmas presents early!

My cart stays empty. Why?
Your browser probably does not accept "cookies", small files that remember what you chose. You can turn them on in your browser's security options. If you don't know how, order by phone.

Can I change my mind?
Of course: you have 10 days from delivery to return the item. Everything is in the terms of sale.

Do you sell second-hand computers?
No, only new items with the manufacturer's warranty. For second-hand we suggest the classified ads and the swap meets, but be careful: a used computer can have anything inside it.
