General terms of sale

1. Who we are. BuyItAll is a mail order service. Orders can be placed on these pages, by phone on our toll-free number, or with the order card in our paper catalog.

2. Prices. All prices are in US dollars and include sales tax. Shipping costs $7 per order, however many items it contains; orders over $250 ship free. Prices may change without notice, but the price on the day of your order always applies.

3. Payment. Cash to the courier on delivery, or by credit card. Card details travel over a secure line and are not kept on our computers once the payment has gone through.

4. Delivery. Usually in 5 to 7 working days, anywhere in the country. The courier calls you the day before. If you are not in, he leaves a note and tries a second time; after the second attempt the parcel goes back to our warehouse.

5. Returns. You have 10 days from delivery to return an item you are not happy with, no questions asked. Send it back in its original packaging with all its accessories: we refund the full price, minus shipping.

6. Warranty. All items carry the manufacturer's warranty of at least 12 months. For computers there is on-site service in major cities; elsewhere we pay for collection and return.

7. Privacy. We use your details only to ship your orders and, if you wish, our catalog. We do not sell them or pass them on to anyone.
