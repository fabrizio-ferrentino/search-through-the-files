How to order

  1. Choose your items and put them in the cart.
  2. Enter your name, address and a daytime phone number.
  3. Choose how to pay: cash on delivery (extra $3) or credit card.
  4. You will receive a confirmation e-mail with your order number. Keep it!
  5. The courier phones you the day before delivery.
