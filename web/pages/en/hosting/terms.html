212 new members		 Rules of the neighbourhood

These rules apply to everybody who has a plot on HomePlot. They are short because we want you to read them. Last changed 03/01/1999.

  1. Your page is yours. You are responsible for what you put on it. We do not read every page, but the mayors do read the ones in the directory.
  2. No adult material of any kind, no violence for its own sake, no pages that offend people for what they are.
  3. No selling. The free plan is for private pages. A page that tries to sell something will be closed after one warning.
  4. Copyright. Do not put on your page text, pictures, songs or programs that belong to somebody else unless they said yes. "I found it on the Web" is not a yes. If you use someone's picture, write their name beside it.
  5. Children. Members under thirteen must have the written permission of a parent, who will receive our welcome letter. Pages by children must not give the home address or telephone number of the child.
  6. Be kind in the guestbooks. Rude messages can be deleted by the page owner or by us.
  7. The banner stays. On the free plan, the HomePlot banner must stay where it is. Taking it off the page is the only offence for which we do not give a warning.
  8. Use the space as intended. No big archives of files to be downloaded by other people. A page that makes the server too slow will be asked to slim down.
  9. One plot for one member. You may have a second one for a club or a school class if you ask.
  10. Inactive plots. A plot that has not been changed or visited for twelve months is closed after a letter to the postal address.
Privacy

We keep the postal address, the year of birth and the page name of each member. We do not sell them or lend them, and we do not know who would want them. The mayors see only the page name. If you ask us in writing, we erase everything about you within thirty days.

Limits of our responsibility

HomePlot is run by a small team on borrowed machines. We do our best to keep your pages on line, but we cannot promise it. If the server breaks and your files are lost, we are sorry and we will help you rebuild, but we cannot pay for the lost evenings. For this reason, please keep a copy of your pages on a floppy disk.
