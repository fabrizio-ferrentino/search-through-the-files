212 new members		 Welcome to HomePlot, the friendly place to put your web page!

41,208 members and growing. Whoever you are and whatever you want to show the world - your dog, your stamps, your band, your recipe for pound cake, the history of your street - HomePlot gives you a plot of your own on the Web, free, with 5 MB of space, an address of your own and a counter. No fee, no contract, no cleverness required.

HomePlot began in the spring of 1996 as a single computer in a spare room in Millbrook, with a free software server and a lot of hope. Now we have eleven servers, six neighbourhoods and a small team that answers your letters on weekdays. We are not part of any large company; we are paid by the banners you see at the top of the page, and by those of our members who choose the Plus plan. Please do not mind the banners: they pay for the electricity.

How it works in three steps
  1. Choose a neighbourhood. Pick the one that fits your page: Rocket Park for computers and science fiction, Paw Street for pets, Melody Lane for music, Garden Row for gardens and cooking, Wheels Avenue for cars and bikes, Study Hall for school and hobbies. See the neighbourhoods page.
  2. Sign up. Fill in the form, choose a name for your address and a password. It takes about two minutes. You get a welcome letter by post within ten days, with your first page already in place.
  3. Build your page. Write it with any editor, or with Notepad, and send it to us by FTP. Read the tips if you have never done it. There is nothing you cannot learn on a rainy afternoon.
