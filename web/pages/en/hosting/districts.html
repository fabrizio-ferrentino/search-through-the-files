212 new members		 The six neighbourhoods

A neighbourhood is a group of pages about the same sort of thing. It helps visitors to find you, and it helps us to move you if one server gets full. You choose it when you sign up, and you can change it once a year by writing to us.

Each neighbourhood has a mayor (a volunteer member) who reads the pages sent to the neighbourhood's directory and tells us if somebody is breaking the rules. The mayors are not paid and are very patient.

A visit to Rocket Park. Most of its pages are made by teenagers who like computers. Typical page: a title in large flashing letters, a picture of a robot, a link to a downloadable game made by the owner, a list of favourite links, and a counter that has not moved for two weeks. Everybody is very polite in the guestbooks.

A visit to Paw Street. Here every page has a photograph of an animal, usually sleeping. The dog pages have a section called "Our walks"; the cat pages have a section called "Our furniture". The mayor of the street, Susanna, is known for having thirteen pets and a very small flat.

A visit to Melody Lane. The young bands of the area keep their pages here, with the dates of their shows, their songs in MIDI and a picture in which the four members look at four different cameras. The mayor is a drummer and requires every band page to have a mailing list.

Neighbourhoods we would like to open: Sports Square, Cinema Corner, Old Town (for local history), and a quiet one called Reading Room. If you want to be mayor of one, write to us.
