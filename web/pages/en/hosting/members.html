A small selection of the pages of our members, updated by hand on 03/20/1999. The addresses are written as they were when we last visited; if a page has moved or been closed, the link will not answer. Names are those the members chose; we do not publish their real names unless they want to. The search service is suspended during the move to the new server.

Jenny K's WorldMelody LaneJenny, fifteen. Her favourite bands, the words of forty songs, and a very long page about her cousin's wedding.

Night Sky NotesRocket ParkA retired electrician with a telescope and a notebook. Charts for the next three months. No link: he does not have a counter and says he does not need one.

Want to be in the list? Send us the address of your page and a line about it. We list pages that have been on line for at least a month and have at least three pages of their own. There is no fee and no vote, only the mayors' judgement and a lot of patience.

Page moved? Write to us and we will change the entry. Pages that no longer answer are removed from the list at the end of the following month.
