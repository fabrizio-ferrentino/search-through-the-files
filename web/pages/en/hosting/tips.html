212 new members		 How to build your first page

A web page is a text file with a few codes in it. You do not need to buy a special program. Here is the shortest page that works:

<html><head><title>My page</title></head><body><h1>Hello</h1><p>This is my page.</p></body></html>

Save it with the name index.html in a folder on your computer and send it to your plot. That is the page that visitors see first. Everything else is a matter of adding things to it.

Ten tips from our mayors
  1. Start small. One good page is better than ten empty ones marked "under construction". Nobody likes the little man with the shovel, and everybody has seen him.
  2. Keep the pictures small. A visitor with a 28.8 modem needs about four seconds for each 10 KB. A background of 80 KB means twenty seconds of grey.
  3. Use readable colours. Yellow text on a white background is a hobby for people who dislike their visitors. If you must use a dark background, use a light text.
  4. Do not use more than two fonts. The page is not a ransom note.
  5. Say what the page is about in the first line, and put your name on it.
  6. Test it with both of the common browsers. They do not agree about tables.
  7. Do not use blinking text for more than a word. A whole page that blinks is a punishment.
  8. Always put a link back to the main page on every page. People arrive from all sorts of places.
  9. Write the date of the last change at the bottom. Visitors like to know if the page is alive.
  10. Back up your files. We back up the server every week, but we are not a miracle.
Uploading by FTP

To send your files to HomePlot you use a program called an FTP client. Any one will do. Give it the name of our server, your page name as the user name, and your password. Then drag the files from the left side of the window (your computer) to the right side (your plot). Remember that the names of the files must be written exactly as they are in your links, and that the server tells capitals from small letters.

If the connection stops halfway, do not worry: send the file again. If the server says "permission denied", you are trying to write in a folder that is not yours; ask your mayor.

What to put in your guestbook

A guestbook is the most used thing on the Web after the counter. Our guestbook form is provided by a script on our server: copy the form from the sample page we send to every new member and change only the text. If a visitor writes something rude, you can ask us to delete it, and we will.

Hit counters. Each plot has one. It counts the page every time it is loaded, including you. A new page with ninety visits in a day is probably you pressing Reload.
