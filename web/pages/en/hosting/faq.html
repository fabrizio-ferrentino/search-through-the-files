212 new members		 Frequently asked questions
Is it really free?
Yes. We make our money from the banners and from the Plus plan. You never receive a bill unless you ask for Plus.

Why a postal address?
To send the welcome letter with the rules and because it keeps away people who sign up for fun and never come back.

How much space do I get?
5 MB for the free plan, 20 MB for Plus. A page of text is about 5 KB, so you can have a thousand. A photograph is about 40 KB, so you can have a hundred and twenty of those.

Can I put music on my page?
MIDI files, yes. MP3 files are too big for the free plan. If you put music, please let the visitor turn it off.

Can I sell things?
No. The free plan is for private pages. If you have a business, ask about Plus and a commercial address.

Can I have my own name, like www.mypage.com?
Not on the free plan. We are studying the idea for Plus, but registering a name costs money and we have not decided how to ask for it.

What is the "500 Internal Server Error" I get when I use a form?
It means the script that handles the form did not answer. During the move to the new server some scripts are switched off. They will come back. Nothing is lost on your side; try again in a few days.

Can I put a counter and a guestbook on every page?
One of each on the main page is enough. More than one slows the server.

My page does not show on the list.
The list is updated by hand at the end of each month. If you have been on line for more than a month and you still do not see your name, write to us.

How many people work at HomePlot?
Five, two of them part-time and one of them the founder's sister.

Do you have a telephone number?
No, because with a telephone we would never write the pages. Letters only, to HomePlot, Mill Street 3, Millbrook.

I forgot my password.
Write to us from the postal address you gave. We will send a new one by letter. It takes about ten days, so write it down this time.

What happens if HomePlot closes?
We will tell you with at least two months' notice and help you to move your files. We do not intend to close; we have just bought a second photocopier.

More questions? Write to HomePlot, Mill Street 3, Millbrook, and put your page name on the envelope.
