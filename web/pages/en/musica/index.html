Welcome to MidiMania! Here you find the MIDI versions of the songs you love, made by our readers with a keyboard, a computer and a lot of evenings. A MIDI file is tiny: a whole song in less than the size of a photo. Your sound card plays it with its own instruments, so it sounds a little different on every computer. That is part of the charm.

The most requested lyrics of March. Thank you to the reader who copied them from the cassette, rewinding forty times.

Kitchen Light - Anna Vale
you left the kitchen light on
so I'd find my way back in
the house was asleep but the light was on
and that's where love begins
Short and perfect. Anna Vale wrote it for her father.

Engines - The Radio Dials
start it up, start it up, it's Saturday
the town is small but the road goes far
turn it up, turn it up, we're on our way
four friends and a borrowed car
Shout the chorus. Everybody does.

We publish only lyrics that readers have transcribed by ear, and we correct them when you tell us we got a word wrong. For Concrete Garden there are currently three versions of the second verse, and nobody agrees.
