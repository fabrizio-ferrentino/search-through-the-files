Welcome to MidiMania! Here you find the MIDI versions of the songs you love, made by our readers with a keyboard, a computer and a lot of evenings. A MIDI file is tiny: a whole song in less than the size of a photo. Your sound card plays it with its own instruments, so it sounds a little different on every computer. That is part of the charm.
