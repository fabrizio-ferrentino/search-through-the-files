How to play a MIDI file

1. You need a sound card. Almost every computer sold in the last three years has one. If your computer beeps but does not play music, you probably do not.

2. Your computer already has a player. Double-click the file: the system's media player opens and plays it. No need to install anything.

3. Why does it sound different from the record? A MIDI file is not a recording: it is a score. It says "play this note, with this instrument, for this long". Your sound card has its own instruments, and a cheap card has cheap instruments. The same file sounds like an orchestra on one computer and like a doorbell on another.

4. Can I put a MIDI on my personal page? Yes, and many people do: the song starts as soon as the page opens. Please choose a quiet song, and keep the volume low. Nobody likes a page that shouts at them at midnight.

5. Can I make my own? Yes, with a keyboard that has a MIDI socket, a cable and a sequencer program. It takes patience. Our best arrangers say the hardest part is not the music, it is knowing when to stop.
