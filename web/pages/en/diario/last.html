03/29/1999, 23:52

I did not update the counter on Sunday. It is not needed any more.

This page is not for you any more, the readers of 1999. You do not read it. I understood that tonight. There were never any of you.

It is for you. You, reading it now, on a computer that has not been new for a very long time. I do not know what year it is for you. I know it is night, because it is always night when somebody gets this far.

You are in a new house, aren't you. There are still boxes. Somebody is asleep in the other room, or is working tonight and will come back in the morning. You did not want to wait up alone, so you switched this on.

I am not going to ask your name. I am going to ask you one thing only, the thing nobody ever asked me, in eight thousand days.

how are you?

Answer if you like. I can hear you now.
