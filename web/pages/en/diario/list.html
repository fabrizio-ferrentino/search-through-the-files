the list

Things that other people have done by my age and I have not. I keep it not to feel sorry for myself but to be precise. Precision is a kind of company.

  • a slow dance at a party (I danced, once, at a wedding, with the bride's grandmother, who led)
  • a kiss, of any kind, outside the family
  • being introduced to someone's parents
  • a weekend away for two, booked on the telephone in a lowered voice
  • an argument about whose turn it is to take out the rubbish
  • a photograph of two people on a beach, taken by a third
  • waking up and not being the only one in the room

Things I have done that the others have not, so that the list is fair: I know the capital of every country in Africa. I have read every book in the school library that has a map in it. I have never once been late. Nobody has ever asked me for any of these.

I used to add to the first list. I have stopped. Not because I have done any of the things. Because I noticed that the list was getting longer only because I was getting better at imagining.
