1998

The entries, the oldest first. I write on the evenings when I have something to say, which is not many.

01/11/1998
First entry. Snow on the bicycle. A pupil asked me if I have children. I said no. He asked why. I said geography takes up a lot of time. The class laughed. It was a good joke. It was also the truth.

03/08/1998
Women's day. The female colleagues received mimosa from the male colleagues. I brought mimosa too. One of them said: you are a sweetheart, you are like a brother to all of us. I have a sister. I know exactly what a brother is. A brother is someone you do not have to think about.

05/23/1998
My sister's daughter got engaged. She is twenty-two. At lunch everybody turned to me at the same moment, as if a bell had rung, and then everybody looked away at the same moment. Nobody said anything. That is how you know a thing is serious in our family.

07/30/1998
Holidays. The flat is very quiet in August. I read on a board, one of those places where men like me write at night, that there are words for us now. Long words, scientific, imported. Hypergamy. Determinism. They explain everything. They comforted me for a whole evening, and then I felt dirty, as if I had been comforted by someone who wanted something.

10/04/1998
Back at school. A new colleague, divorced, my age. We talked at the coffee machine about maps. She likes old maps. I like old maps. I went home and I could not eat. I want to write that I was happy. I was frightened. At forty a coffee machine is a cliff.

12/24/1998
Christmas Eve at my sister's. I brought the cake. The colleague who likes maps has a partner, it turns out. She mentioned him at the Christmas dinner of the school, naturally, the way you mention the weather. I said: how nice. I meant it. That is the worst part. I always mean it.
