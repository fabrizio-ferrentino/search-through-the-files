1999

The entries, the oldest first. I write on the evenings when I have something to say, which is not many.

01/17/1999
Counter: 8,036 when I started this page, and every Sunday seven more. I realised tonight that I add the seven before I go to bed, like brushing my teeth. A man who brushes his teeth and adds seven. That is a whole biography.

02/14/1999
A pupil left a valentine on my desk as a joke. A drawing of a heart with a ruler through it, because I always carry a ruler for the maps. The class waited for me to get angry. I put it in my bag. I still have it. It is the first one.

03/07/1999
I have stopped reading the board. The long words were explaining everything and changing nothing, and slowly they were changing me. I caught myself looking at the colleague who likes maps and thinking a percentage. I put the thought down like you put down something hot. I do not want to be a man who thinks percentages about people.

03/21/1999
Spring. The light comes back into the classroom at the last lesson. I do not have anything to report, and I wanted to write that too: a day without anything is still a day. Seven more on Sunday.

a personal page. no guestbook, no e-mail. it is enough that you read.
