On January 1, 2000, at midnight, many computers will think it is the year 1900. This is not a joke, and it is not science fiction. It is a plain fact, known to engineers since the 1970s, which the newspapers discovered about two years ago: for decades programmers wrote the year with two digits to save memory, when memory was expensive. "99" means 1999. But what comes after "99"? For a computer with two digits, it is "00". And "00" can be read as 1900.

Nobody knows exactly what will happen. Some experts say "nothing", some say "a few annoying problems", and a few say "the end of the world". I am an ordinary person and I do not know. What I know is that I would rather be ready for nothing than be unready for something. That is why I wrote this page.

DO NOT PANIC. BUT DO NOT SLEEP EITHER.

The five things everybody should do now
  1. Ask the makers of your computer, video recorder and car whether they are "Year 2000 compliant". Write down the answer, with the name of the person who gave it.
  2. Test your computer: set the date to December 31, 1999, 11:58 PM, wait for midnight, and see what the machine says. Before doing it, save everything on a floppy disk.
  3. Take a printout of every bank statement and every insurance paper this year. A copy on paper is a copy that does not need a clock.
  4. Buy some candles, a gas stove for camping, and water. A week is enough. Do not buy a generator unless you really need one.
  5. Talk to your neighbours. The best protection is having a neighbour who knows your name.
