About me and this page

My name is Roy Dalton. I am fifty-four, I live in Millbrook, and until 1997 I was a heating engineer. I am not a computer expert. I own one computer, bought in 1996, and I learned to make a web page from a book from the library that had three pages torn out.

How it started. In January 1998 I saw a programme on television about the year 2000 problem. The man on the screen said that "nobody knows". I thought: a boiler engineer knows that when a machine has a clock, somebody must have set the clock. And when it counts years with two digits, somebody must have decided what happens at the end. I went to the cellar, found the control panel of my boiler, and read the booklet. It said nothing. That was when I got worried.

Why a web page? Because my wife told me that if I talked about it once more at dinner she would leave. I put it on the Web so that the people who want to hear can find it, and the ones who do not can close the window. It is a good arrangement.

What I do not know
  • Whether the power stations are ready. The company says yes. I would like to see the cellar.
  • Whether the traffic lights are ready. I know where the box is, and I looked at it. It did not tell me much.
  • What the newspapers will say on January 2.
Myths and facts
People sayI think
"All computers will stop."No. Many will carry on; some will give wrong dates; very few will stop.
"Aeroplanes will fall."Planes fly with several systems, and many have no date in them. I will still take the train.
"It is a hoax."It is not. But it is not the end of the world either. The truth is boring.
"Only old computers are at risk."Not true: some new ones have the same problem, because they use old programs.
"The government will fix it."It would be nice. It has much to fix. Help it by fixing your own things.

Contact. Use the form on the letters page, or leave a note for me at the Millbrook library. I answer every letter, as long as it does not contain the words "stupid old man".

Thank you to the librarian, who found me the book, to my neighbour who let me test his cash register, to my wife, who has not left, and to my cat, who has no opinions about the year 2000 and no wish to have any.
