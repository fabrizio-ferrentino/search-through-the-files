Away trips

HILLCREST - GRIFFINS, Sunday 04/18/1999
Coach leaving from the station square at 11:30. Back around 8 PM. Fifty places, thirty-one taken. Bookings at the Station Bar, from Gino the barman (not Mister Gino, the other Gino).

Rules of the coach (yes, there are rules, after Southbay):

  1. The coach leaves at the time written. Not five minutes later. The driver has a family.
  2. Songs are allowed. Songs against the driver are not.
  3. Whatever you bring to eat, bring enough for your neighbour.
  4. Whoever is sick on the coach pays for the cleaning. It happened once. Once was enough.
  5. On the way back, if we lost, silence until the motorway exit. Then we start singing again. Always.

The best away trip ever: the one to Southbay in February, 0-2, with the coach that broke down on the way back and forty people singing in a motorway service area at midnight. The barista there still remembers us. We sent him a scarf.
