AWAY TRIPS!!!

HILLCREST - GRIFFINS, Sunday 04/18/1999
Coach leaving from the station square at 11:30. Back around 8 PM. Fifty places, thirty-one taken. Bookings at the Station Bar, from Gino the barman (not Mister Gino, the other Gino).

Rules of the coach (yes, there are rules, after Southbay):

  1. The coach leaves at the time written. Not five minutes later. The driver has a family.
  2. Songs are allowed. Songs against the driver are not.
  3. Whatever you bring to eat, bring enough for your neighbour.
  4. Whoever is sick on the coach pays for the cleaning. It happened once. Once was enough.
  5. On the way back, if we lost, silence until the motorway exit. Then we start singing again. Always.

The best away trip ever: the one to Southbay in February, 0-2, with the coach that broke down on the way back and forty people singing in a motorway service area at midnight. The barista there still remembers us. We sent him a scarf.

OUR HISTORY!

The Griffins were founded in 1946 by the workers of the brick factory, who played on the field behind the kilns. The name comes from the stone griffin above the door of the town hall, which the founders saw every morning on their way to work. The colours, red and blue, come from the only set of shirts they could afford: a donation from a team in the city that had changed its colours.

The great years: 1962, the only season in the third division, and the cup final of 1979, lost on penalties in front of four thousand people - half the town. Those who were there still talk about it; those who were not say they were.

JOIN THE FAN CLUB!!!

The card costs $15 a season ($8 for under-fourteens and over-seventies) and gives you: a discount on the ticket for home matches, a place on the coach for the away trips, the fan club scarf (the first year), and the right to vote at the annual meeting, where every year we vote on whether to change the drum.

How to join: at the Station Bar, any day after 5 PM, or at the stadium before home matches, at the table under the flag. Online membership does not exist and will not exist: we want to see your face.

What we ask of members: support the team, always, even when they lose; respect the other team's fans, who are people from the next town and probably your cousins; and never throw anything onto the pitch. Last season someone threw a sandwich. The fine came out of the fan club's money.
