JOIN THE FAN CLUB!!!

The card costs $15 a season ($8 for under-fourteens and over-seventies) and gives you: a discount on the ticket for home matches, a place on the coach for the away trips, the fan club scarf (the first year), and the right to vote at the annual meeting, where every year we vote on whether to change the drum.

How to join: at the Station Bar, any day after 5 PM, or at the stadium before home matches, at the table under the flag. Online membership does not exist and will not exist: we want to see your face.

What we ask of members: support the team, always, even when they lose; respect the other team's fans, who are people from the next town and probably your cousins; and never throw anything onto the pitch. Last season someone threw a sandwich. The fine came out of the fan club's money.

Our yearly events: the dinner in February, the end of season party in June with the players, the Christmas tombola, and the trip to see a big match in the city, every spring.
