Our history

The Griffins were founded in 1946 by the workers of the brick factory, who played on the field behind the kilns. The name comes from the stone griffin above the door of the town hall, which the founders saw every morning on their way to work. The colours, red and blue, come from the only set of shirts they could afford: a donation from a team in the city that had changed its colours.

The great years: 1962, the only season in the third division, and the cup final of 1979, lost on penalties in front of four thousand people - half the town. Those who were there still talk about it; those who were not say they were.

The fan club was born in 1974, in the back room of the Station Bar, where it still has its seat. The founding members were eleven, "like a team". Today we are about two hundred, from eight to eighty-nine years old: our eldest member, Mr Aldo, has not missed a home match since 1951 and still brings his own folding chair.

Our motto - "in the rain and in the mud" - comes from a match in 1981 played in a storm, with the pitch under water and the ball that would not roll. The Griffins won 1-0 with a goal scored, the legend says, by pushing the ball across the line with the knees. Nobody has the photographs. Everybody remembers.
