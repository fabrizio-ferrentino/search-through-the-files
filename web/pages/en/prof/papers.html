Publications

Journal articles are listed first, then technical reports, then conference talks. Reprints are available from the author on request; I have a box of them under the desk. Please write your name and address on a card and leave it at the department office.

Journal articles

  1. W. Brandt, On the convergence of block iterative methods for sparse symmetric systems, Annals of Applied Computation 14 (1981), pp. 211-229.
  2. W. Brandt and L. Okafor, A note on pivoting strategies for banded matrices, Journal of Computational Methods 6 (1984), pp. 87-95.
  3. W. Brandt, Error bounds for the trapezoid rule in the presence of singularities, Annals of Applied Computation 19 (1986), pp. 301-316.
  4. W. Brandt, M. Eklund and P. Santini, Stability regions of some implicit multistep formulas, Journal of Computational Methods 11 (1989), pp. 140-162.
  5. W. Brandt, A stiffness detector for small computers, Computing Letters 3 (1992), pp. 19-27.
  6. W. Brandt and M. Eklund, Accuracy of linear solvers on machines without a hardware multiplier, Journal of Computational Methods 17 (1995), pp. 5-21.
  7. W. Brandt, What is a good tolerance? Remarks on stopping criteria, Annals of Applied Computation 31 (1997), pp. 55-68.
