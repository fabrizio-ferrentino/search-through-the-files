Questions I am asked often

Can I do the oral exam before the written one?
No.
Can I use a programmable calculator?
You can use it for a project. You cannot use it at the exam.
May I hand in the project late?
Yes, until the day of the oral exam. After that it counts for the next session.
Is the course going to change next year?
A little. I want to add a lecture on the double precision format, now that every machine has it.
Do you have the old exams?
The last five years are in the library, on the reference shelf, in a grey folder. Answers are not included, on purpose.

For other universities

If you are a student at another university and have a question about my papers, please write by post and enclose a stamped envelope. If you want to invite me to a conference, thank you, and please give me at least four months' notice and the name of the hotel.
