Welcome

I am a professor of numerical analysis in the Department of Mathematics of Lakeland University, in Millbrook. My office is in the Hobbs Building, second floor, between the photocopier and the broken coffee machine. I have been here since 1984. Before that I was a research assistant at the Institute of Applied Computation, and before that a student, like everybody else.

These pages contain what students usually ask me for: the syllabus of my courses, the exam dates, the notes of my lectures, the list of my papers and the times when I can be found in my office. They are not meant to be pretty. If you look for something flashy, this is not the place.

Research interests

  • Numerical solution of linear systems, in particular iterative methods for sparse matrices
  • Error analysis and stability of floating-point computation
  • Numerical methods for ordinary differential equations (stiff problems)
  • Teaching numerical methods with small computers: what a student can learn on a machine with 8 MB of memory
