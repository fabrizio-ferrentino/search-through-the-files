Welcome to the shelter

Forty-two dogs, twenty-eight cats, one rabbit that nobody remembers bringing in, and twelve volunteers who come every day, in the rain and the snow, to feed them, walk them and talk to them. Faithful Friend is a volunteer association: we live on donations and on the love of people who adopt.

Our oldest guest is Grey, a dog who has been with us for six years. Everybody walks past his kennel because he is old and does not jump. If you have a quiet house and a lot of patience, he is the best dog you will ever meet.
