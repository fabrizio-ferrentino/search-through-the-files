Welcome to the shelter

Forty-two dogs, twenty-eight cats, one rabbit that nobody remembers bringing in, and twelve volunteers who come every day, in the rain and the snow, to feed them, walk them and talk to them. Faithful Friend is a volunteer association: we live on donations and on the love of people who adopt.

How to adopt

Step 1 Come to the shelter, on Saturday or Sunday from 10 AM to 5 PM, or on weekdays by appointment. Bring the whole family: the dog must like everyone, and everyone must like the dog.
Step 2 Meet the animals with a volunteer, who knows each one's character. Take your time. Come back a second time if you are not sure.
Step 3 A conversation: where you live, how many hours the animal will be alone, who will walk it. It is not an exam. It is so that the animal does not come back.
Step 4 The adoption form, signed at the shelter, with an identity document. Adoption is free; a donation is welcome.
Step 5 A visit from a volunteer a few weeks later, to see how it is going. Most of the time we are offered coffee and shown photographs.

Please think before adopting. A dog lives twelve or fifteen years. Every summer, when the holidays start, the shelter fills up with animals that were a Christmas present. We do not judge anyone. We only ask you to think.

How to help us

Blankets, towels, old sheets: always. Bring them to the shelter or leave them at the vet's in the main street.
Spread the word: tell your friends about Grey, about Shadow, about the six puppies. Most adoptions start with someone saying "I know a dog who...".

Last year, thanks to you, we built a new covered area for the dogs and paid the vet's bills for the whole winter. Thank you.

Become a volunteer

You do not need to be an expert. You need two hours a week, old clothes, and no fear of mud. Our volunteers are students, pensioners, a nurse who comes after the night shift, and a retired policeman who is the only one the big dogs obey.

Just come on a Saturday morning and ask for the person with the wheelbarrow.
