Happy endings

Bella, adopted 02/1999
"We came for a puppy and went home with Bella, eight years old, because she put her head on my son's knee and did not move it. She sleeps at the foot of his bed. When he has a bad dream, she wakes him up". - a family from the new estate

Socks, adopted 12/1998
"I live alone since my wife died. My daughter said: take a cat, Dad. I said no. She brought me to the shelter anyway. Now Socks waits for me at the window, and I have someone to say good morning to". - Mr Walter

Rocky, adopted 06/1997
"Rocky is the real boss of our house", wrote a boy of sixteen on his personal page. We agree: we saw the photographs.

Send us your adopted animal's story and a photograph. We pin them all on the board in the shelter's office, so the ones still waiting can see that it happens.
