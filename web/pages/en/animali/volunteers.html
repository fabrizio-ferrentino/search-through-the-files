Become a volunteer

You do not need to be an expert. You need two hours a week, old clothes, and no fear of mud. Our volunteers are students, pensioners, a nurse who comes after the night shift, and a retired policeman who is the only one the big dogs obey.

What volunteers do: walk the dogs, clean the kennels, brush the cats, wash the bowls, fix the fences, answer the phone, and - the most important thing - spend time with the animals. A dog that is walked every day is adopted sooner, because it is calmer and happier when people come.

Who can come: everyone over sixteen. Under sixteen, with a parent. Every new volunteer spends the first Saturday with an expert one.

Several of our volunteers told us that they came for the animals and stayed for the people. Lonely people find company here, on both sides of the fence.
