How to help us

Blankets, towels, old sheets: always. Bring them to the shelter or leave them at the vet's in the main street.
Food: dry food for dogs and cats, any brand. The supermarket in the square has a basket by the exit.
Money: by postal order to the association, or in the box at the shelter. Online donations are not possible: we do not have a bank that does them.
Your time: see volunteers.
Spread the word: tell your friends about Grey, about Shadow, about the six puppies. Most adoptions start with someone saying "I know a dog who...".

Last year, thanks to you, we built a new covered area for the dogs and paid the vet's bills for the whole winter. Thank you.
