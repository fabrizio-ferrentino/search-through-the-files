How to adopt

  1. Come to the shelter, on Saturday or Sunday from 10 AM to 5 PM, or on weekdays by appointment. Bring the whole family: the dog must like everyone, and everyone must like the dog.
  2. Meet the animals with a volunteer, who knows each one's character. Take your time. Come back a second time if you are not sure.
  3. A conversation: where you live, how many hours the animal will be alone, who will walk it. It is not an exam. It is so that the animal does not come back.
  4. The adoption form, signed at the shelter, with an identity document. Adoption is free; a donation is welcome.
  5. A visit from a volunteer a few weeks later, to see how it is going. Most of the time we are offered coffee and shown photographs.

Please think before adopting. A dog lives twelve or fifteen years. Every summer, when the holidays start, the shelter fills up with animals that were a Christmas present. We do not judge anyone. We only ask you to think.

Adoption by Internet is not possible, and never will be: an animal is not a parcel.
