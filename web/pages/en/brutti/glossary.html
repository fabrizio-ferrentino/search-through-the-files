Glossary

The words we use here. If you have been on the board for a month you know them. If you have been here for a year you are tired of them.

  • hypergamy: the rule by which women always choose upwards: a man with more looks, more money, more status than they have. Nobody here invented it. We only stopped pretending it is not there.
  • genetic determinism: what you are was decided before you were born. The jaw, the height, the eyes. Courses, gyms and methods only move the furniture around in a house that is already built.
  • the 80/20: eighty percent of women chase twenty percent of men. The other eighty percent of men share what is left, which is nothing. TheProfessor has the numbers.
  • LMS: looks, money, status. The three things that count. Personality is what they say counts when they are not choosing.
  • the wall: the age after which, according to some members, everything changes for women. We do not use this word to laugh. Or we try not to.
  • the black pill: the moment you stop believing that you can fix it. Some say it is freedom. Some say it is a room with no door.
  • it is over: what you write when you have nothing else to write. Please write something else too.
  • normal: a man who has had a girlfriend, a dance, a first kiss at fifteen, and therefore does not understand anything we say.
