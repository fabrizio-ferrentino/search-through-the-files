My face measured: 3.9 out of 10

Posted by bignose78 on 06/22/1998 at 03:15:09

I did it. The ruler, the mirror, the calculator, the whole method from the face site. Two evenings. Distance between the eyes, the length of the nose, the angle of the jaw, the famous ratio. Result: 3.9.

The site says that under 4 the 'market response is statistically absent'. I printed the table and put it in the drawer with my school reports. Same drawer. It seemed right.

The strange thing is that I am calm. For years I thought: maybe tomorrow. Now there is no tomorrow to wait for. There is a number.
