Hypergamy explained to the new ones

Posted by TheProfessor on 01/17/1998 at 23:40:02

Every month somebody new arrives asking the same question: what am I doing wrong. So here it is once, for everybody.

You are not doing anything wrong. A woman, like every animal, chooses the best she can get. Upwards. Never sideways, never down. This is hypergamy. In the past society forced the numbers to come out even: a village, a church, a man for every woman. Now it does not. Now there are cars, telephones, and soon the Internet, and every woman can see the top twenty percent of men from her bedroom. Why should she look at you?

I have collected numbers on this for two years. They are on my page; I will not link it, the rules, but everybody knows where it is. The numbers do not lie. People lie. Numbers do not.
