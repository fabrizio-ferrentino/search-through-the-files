Stop asking what you are doing wrong

Posted by TheProfessor on 01/17/1998 at 23:40:02

Every month somebody new arrives asking the same question: what am I doing wrong. I am tired of answering it one by one.

You are not doing anything wrong. Hypergamy, determinism, the 80/20: you have read the words here a hundred times, you know them better than your own name by now. My grandparents had a village, a church and one dance a year, and the numbers came out even. You have cars, telephones and soon the Internet, and every woman can see the best men in the country from her bedroom. Why should she look at you?

I have collected numbers on this for two years. They are on my page; I will not link it, the rules, but everybody knows where it is. The numbers do not lie. People lie. Numbers do not.
