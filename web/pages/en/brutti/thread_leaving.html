I am leaving the board

Posted by sandpaper on 09/14/1998 at 22:48:30

I have been reading here every night for a year. I want to say goodbye properly because you were the only people I talked to for a long time, and that counts.

But I noticed something. When I came here I was sad. Now I am sad and I have numbers for it. I look at the girl at the bakery and I do not see a girl, I see a percentage. I look at my parents and I see an exception. I measured my own father with a ruler. That is not honesty. That is a disease and we pass it to each other every night.

I have signed up for a Thursday evening class, ballroom dancing, at the community centre. I will probably be terrible. There are eleven women and four men and the teacher is seventy. I am going anyway. If it goes badly I will be sad without numbers, which is the way people used to be sad.
