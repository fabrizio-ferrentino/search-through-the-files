Read before posting

1. Honesty first. If someone asks whether he is ugly, tell him. Lying to us is what everybody else already does.

2. No photos. The server is small and so are our hearts. Describe yourself in words, or use the measurements from the face site everybody here already knows.

3. Nobody comes here to be cheered up. If you want to be cheered up, there are a thousand sites with sunsets on them.

4. Do not insult other members. Insult the world if you need to, the world can take it.

5. Do not post your address, your phone number or the name of your town. Some of us have been found by people from work. It did not end well.

6. If you are thinking of doing something stupid, write it here first. Somebody will answer. Usually.

7. Women may read but may not post. It is not hatred. It is that this is the one place where we do not have to perform.

- BrokenMirror, administrator, 10/1997

A note added in 1998: the moderators are two people with jobs. We read everything, but not at once. If a message stays without an answer for a week, it is not because nobody cares. It is because nobody was there.
