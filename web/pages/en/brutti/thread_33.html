33 and never kissed a girl. Normal?

Posted by thirtythree_zero on 04/03/1998 at 01:02:37

The title says it all. Thirty-three. Never a kiss, never a hand held, never a date that was a date. I am not a monster. I have a job at the post office, a car, a cat. I read. I cook a decent risotto.

The problem is the time. At twenty it was shyness. At twenty-five it was bad luck. At thirty-three it is a fact about me, like a scar. If I met someone tomorrow, how would I tell her? At my age they expect you to know things. They expect you to have a past. I do not have a past. I have a long present.

Is there anyone else here like me? Not almost like me. Exactly like me.
