First courses

  • Sunday lasagne - for when the whole family comes

Broth for when you are ill. A piece of beef, a piece of chicken, a carrot, a celery stick, an onion with a clove stuck in it, cold water, salt. On the smallest flame for three hours, without boiling: it must smile, not laugh. Skim it. Strain it. Cook small pasta in it, and eat it in bed with a blanket. It does not cure anything, but it helps everything.

Pasta and beans. The night before, put the dried beans in water. The next day, cook them slowly with a potato, a little tomato, a rosemary sprig. Mash half of them with a fork, so the soup becomes thick. Add short pasta and cook it inside. Pepper, a drop of oil on top. On the second day it is better.

Spaghetti with nothing. For when it is late and the fridge is empty: garlic, oil, a chilli if you like it, parsley if you have it. While the water boils you make the sauce in a pan. My husband called it "the midnight spaghetti" because he made them for me when we came home late from dancing, sixty years ago. It is the only recipe he ever knew.
