My mother's Easter cake

For a big round tin

  • for the pastry: five hundred grams of flour, two hundred of sugar, two hundred of butter, three eggs, a lemon
  • for the filling: four hundred grams of ricotta, three hundred of sugar, four eggs, a cup of boiled wheat, candied orange peel, orange-blossom water
  • for the top: strips of pastry, icing sugar

My mother made it on Holy Thursday, and on Holy Thursday I make it, because it needs two days to rest before Easter. The whole house waited for it: the smell of orange blossom came out from under the cloth and reached the street.

  1. The pastry. Work the butter with the sugar, add the eggs and the lemon zest, then the flour, quickly, with cold hands. Wrap it in a cloth and leave it in the cool for an hour.
  2. The filling. Beat the ricotta with the sugar until it is smooth, add the eggs one at a time, then the wheat, the peel cut small and a spoonful of orange-blossom water. Only one: too much and it tastes of soap.
  3. Line the tin with two thirds of the pastry, pour in the filling, and with the rest make strips to lay on top in a lattice. In the oven at 170 degrees for an hour and a half, until it is golden. Leave it to cool in the oven with the door open.

My mother put a coin under one of the strips, and whoever found it would have a lucky year. One year it was my brother, and he swallowed it. He had a lucky year anyway.
