Main courses

Chicken with potatoes, as on Sundays in the country. Chicken in pieces, potatoes in pieces, rosemary, sage, garlic in its skin, oil, salt. Everything in the same tin, in the oven, for an hour and a quarter. Turn everything over halfway. The potatoes under the chicken are the best part, and in my house there was always a fight for them.

Meatballs. Minced meat, a slice of stale bread soaked in milk and squeezed, an egg, cheese, parsley, a little garlic, salt. Mix with your hands - not with a spoon, with your hands, the meat must feel that someone is there. Make balls the size of a walnut. Fry them, or cook them in tomato sauce, or both: first fried, then in the sauce. That is how my mother did them, and that is how they disappear before they reach the table.

Fish on Fridays. When I was a girl we ate fish on Friday because it was Friday. Now I eat it because it is good. Cod with potatoes and olives, in the oven, half an hour. Or fried anchovies, if someone else cleans them.

Omelette with what is left. Six eggs, the leftover vegetables of the day before, cheese. In the pan on a low flame with a lid. To turn it, a plate on top and courage. If it breaks, call it scrambled eggs and nobody will know.
