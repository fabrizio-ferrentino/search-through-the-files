Good morning, or good evening, depending on when you read me. My name is Rose, I am seventy-eight years old, and this is my kitchen on the computer. My grandson Luke made it for me at Christmas, because I was always copying the recipes by hand for everyone and my wrist hurt.

Here you will find the recipes of my family, the ones I learned from my mother and the ones I invented when there was nothing in the house. They are written the way I make them. When I say "a little", it means a little. When I say "until it is ready", it means you have to look at it.

  • The recipe of the week: Sunday lasagne, the one that takes all morning and is eaten in ten minutes.
  • Easter is coming: the Easter cake of my mother, with the eggs in the dough.

If you try a recipe, write to me and tell me how it went. Luke reads me the letters on Sunday when he comes to lunch, and then I answer, and he writes. You can read some of them in your letters.

A kitchen where someone cooks is never empty.

P.S. from Luke: I drew the steaming pot myself, and Grandma says the soup in it is too thin. Page updated on 03/28/1999.
