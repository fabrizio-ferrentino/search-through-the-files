The computer room
Twelve computers, one printer, one scanner and a modem that connects the whole room to the Internet through a single phone line. When more than four students are online at the same time, the pages arrive one line at a time. We are working on it. (We are waiting for the money.)

What we do: word processing and spreadsheets for everyone in the first two years; programming in the third and fourth years of the science track; and, on Wednesday afternoons, the computer club, which made this site.

The rules of the room: no food, no drinks, no games during lessons, no changing the desktop wallpaper (it will be changed back), and no chat. The chat rule is not because chat is evil. It is because we have one phone line and twelve students who would all like to talk to somebody far away rather than to the person sitting next to them.

The computer club is looking for new members, especially from the first years. You do not need to know anything. You need to be curious and to come on Wednesdays.
