Contacts

Secretary's office: open to the public from Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 12 PM. In the afternoon only by appointment.

The head teacher receives parents on Tuesday mornings, by appointment through the secretary's office.

The listening desk: Monday mornings, with the school psychologist. For students, and for parents who are worried about a son or a daughter. Everything said there stays there.

The computer club: Wednesday afternoons, computer room, ground floor, the door with the sign "knock, we are thinking".

The school does not answer e-mails about marks. Marks are discussed in person, at the parents' evenings.
