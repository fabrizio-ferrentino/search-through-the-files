The Galileo Gazette
March 1999 issue

A band with a name at last - by the editors
The Loud Neighbours (fourth year) have finally chosen a name, after eight months. They are looking for a singer. Requirements, according to the bass player: "a voice, a little patience, and not being our drummer".

The canteen survey - by 2B
We asked 200 students what they think of the canteen. Results: pasta 7/10, the Friday fish 3/10, the chocolate pudding 11/10 (some students gave it more than ten, we counted it anyway).

Is anyone lonely here? - by a fifth-year student, who asked not to sign
We are 480 in this building every morning, and I think some of us go home every afternoon without having really spoken to anyone. I am not complaining. I just noticed. If you are one of them, sit with us at the bench by the gym at break. We do not bite. Well, Fred bites, but only sandwiches.

Millennium bug: will the school bell ring in January 2000? - by the computer club
We checked. The bell is not connected to a computer. It is connected to the caretaker, who has assured us that he is millennium-compliant.

The Galileo Gazette is written by the students. Articles can be left in the editors' box in the library. We publish everything that is signed, true and not offensive. Unsigned pieces only if we think they matter.
