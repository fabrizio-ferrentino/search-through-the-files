School trips 1998/99

First years
One day at the science museum in the city, 04/20/1999.

Second years
The nature reserve on the coast, 04/28/1999, with the biology teachers.

Third years
Two days in the capital of art, 05/06/1999 and 05/07/1999.

Fourth years
Three days in the mountains, from 03/22/1999 to 03/24/1999. Done. Survived.

Fifth years
Five days abroad, from 04/19/1999. Destination voted by the students: the capital across the sea.

The fourth-year trip told by a student: "Three days in the snow, a hotel where the heating worked only in the corridor, a cable car to the top and a whole class quiet for once, listening to a classmate play the guitar after dinner. The Latin teacher cried. We will deny it if asked".

Trip authorisations must be signed by a parent and handed in to the class coordinator at least fifteen days before departure. Students who do not take part in the trip attend lessons as usual, in the classes that stay at school.
