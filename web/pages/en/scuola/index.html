Welcome
Millbrook High School has 480 students, 52 teachers, 21 classes, a gym that leaks when it rains and, since last year, a computer room with twelve machines connected to the Internet. This site is made by the students of the computer club, who meet on Wednesday afternoons and are always looking for new members.
