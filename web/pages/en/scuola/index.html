Welcome
Millbrook High School has 480 students, 52 teachers, 21 classes, a gym that leaks when it rains and, since last year, a computer room with twelve machines connected to the Internet. This site is made by the students of the computer club, who meet on Wednesday afternoons and are always looking for new members.

The computer room
Twelve computers, one printer, one scanner and a modem that connects the whole room to the Internet through a single phone line. When more than four students are online at the same time, the pages arrive one line at a time. We are working on it. (We are waiting for the money.)

What we do: word processing and spreadsheets for everyone in the first two years; programming in the third and fourth years of the science track; and, on Wednesday afternoons, the computer club, which made this site.

The computer club is looking for new members, especially from the first years. You do not need to know anything. You need to be curious and to come on Wednesdays.

Contacts

Secretary's office: open to the public from Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 12 PM. In the afternoon only by appointment.

The head teacher receives parents on Tuesday mornings, by appointment through the secretary's office.

The listening desk: Monday mornings, with the school psychologist. For students, and for parents who are worried about a son or a daughter. Everything said there stays there.

The computer club: Wednesday afternoons, computer room, ground floor, the door with the sign "knock, we are thinking".

The school does not answer e-mails about marks. Marks are discussed in person, at the parents' evenings.

The Galileo Gazette
March 1999 issue

A band with a name at last - by the editors
The Loud Neighbours (fourth year) have finally chosen a name, after eight months. They are looking for a singer. Requirements, according to the bass player: "a voice, a little patience, and not being our drummer".

The canteen survey - by 2B
We asked 200 students what they think of the canteen. Results: pasta 7/10, the Friday fish 3/10, the chocolate pudding 11/10 (some students gave it more than ten, we counted it anyway).

Is anyone lonely here? - by a fifth-year student, who asked not to sign
We are 480 in this building every morning, and I think some of us go home every afternoon without having really spoken to anyone. I am not complaining. I just noticed. If you are one of them, sit with us at the bench by the gym at break. We do not bite. Well, Fred bites, but only sandwiches.

Millennium bug: will the school bell ring in January 2000? - by the computer club
We checked. The bell is not connected to a computer. It is connected to the caretaker, who has assured us that he is millennium-compliant.
