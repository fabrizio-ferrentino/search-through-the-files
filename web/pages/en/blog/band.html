THE LOUD NEIGHBOURS

YES. After eight months of arguments we have a name. The Loud Neighbours, because Fred's neighbour calls his mum every Saturday to complain. We thought it was only fair to thank her.

THE LINE-UP: Tommy (guitar), Fred (drums, and he still wants to sing), me (bass), and for the moment no singer. We are looking for one. Requirements: a voice, a little patience, and not being Fred.

WHERE WE PLAY: Fred's garage, every Saturday from three to six, until his neighbour calls.

OUR SONGS: we have four of our own. "Bench in the Square" (about the gang), "Phone Bill" (about my dad), "September" (slow, Tommy wrote it, it is actually good) and "Untitled 4" (it doesn't have a title yet, it doesn't have words either).

FIRST CONCERT: the end of school party, in June, if the head teacher says yes. He hasn't said no. Fred says that means yes. Tommy says that means he hasn't read the letter yet.

Our page will be here when Fred finishes it. Don't hold your breath.
