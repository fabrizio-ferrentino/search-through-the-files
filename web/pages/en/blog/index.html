ABOUT ME

ME ME ME!!

NAME: Jack (nobody calls me by my real name except the teachers and my grandmother)

AGE: 16 and three quarters

SCHOOL: fourth year, science track. Maths ok, Latin a disaster, PE the best hour of the week. My dream is to become a sound engineer. Or a bass player. Or both. My dad says first the diploma.

LIKES: music (loud), pizza (with everything), the sea in September when there is nobody, my bass (it's red), staying up late on the phone with Sarah even though it costs a fortune and Dad goes crazy when the bill arrives.

DOESN'T LIKE: Latin, mushrooms, when the modem disconnects in the middle of something, Fred when he sings.

FAMILY: Mum, Dad, my little sister (8, she says this page is boring because there are no horses) and Rocky, the dog, who is the real boss of the house.

MY COMPUTER: a Pentium 166 with 32 MB of RAM that I share with the whole family. I get it after dinner, after Dad has done the accounts and my sister has done her coloring game. So basically from ten to midnight. That is why the page grows slowly.

THE BAND

THE LOUD NEIGHBOURS

YES. After eight months of arguments we have a name. The Loud Neighbours, because Fred's neighbour calls his mum every Saturday to complain. We thought it was only fair to thank her.

THE LINE-UP: Tommy (guitar), Fred (drums, and he still wants to sing), me (bass), and for the moment no singer. We are looking for one. Requirements: a voice, a little patience, and not being Fred.

WHERE WE PLAY: Fred's garage, every Saturday from three to six, until his neighbour calls.

OUR SONGS: we have four of our own. "Bench in the Square" (about the gang), "Phone Bill" (about my dad), "September" (slow, Tommy wrote it, it is actually good) and "Untitled 4" (it doesn't have a title yet, it doesn't have words either).

FIRST CONCERT: the end of school party, in June, if the head teacher says yes. He hasn't said no. Fred says that means yes. Tommy says that means he hasn't read the letter yet.

Our page will be here when Fred finishes it. Don't hold your breath.

SCHOOL TRIP

NEW!!

THREE DAYS IN THE MOUNTAINS - fourth year - 03/22/1999 to 03/24/1999

The photos are on paper for now, so here are the descriptions. Imagine them. Use your fantasy. (The scanner is coming back from Germany in April.)

  1. The coach at six in the morning. Everyone asleep except Fred, who is singing.
  2. The hotel. Three stars on the sign, one star in reality. The heating worked only in the corridor.
  3. Sarah and Lucy in the snow. Lucy is throwing a snowball at the camera. The camera survived. I did not.
  4. Tommy playing the guitar in the hotel lounge after dinner. The whole class around him, even the teachers. The Latin teacher was CRYING. I am not making it up.
  5. The night of the second day. Nobody slept. Room 14 vs room 16, pillow war. Room 14 won. I was in room 16.
  6. The last morning. The view from the top of the cable car. Everything white down to the lake. Fred finally silent, because of the cold.
  7. The coach back. Everyone asleep. Even Fred.

Best trip ever. Next year, the last one, they say we go abroad. If they do not ban us after room 16.
