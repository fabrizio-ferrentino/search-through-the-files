THREE DAYS IN THE MOUNTAINS - fourth year - 03/22/1999 to 03/24/1999

The photos are on paper for now, so here are the descriptions. Imagine them. Use your fantasy. (The scanner is coming back from Germany in April.)

  1. The coach at six in the morning. Everyone asleep except Fred, who is singing.
  2. The hotel. Three stars on the sign, one star in reality. The heating worked only in the corridor.
  3. Sarah and Lucy in the snow. Lucy is throwing a snowball at the camera. The camera survived. I did not.
  4. Tommy playing the guitar in the hotel lounge after dinner. The whole class around him, even the teachers. The Latin teacher was CRYING. I am not making it up.
  5. The night of the second day. Nobody slept. Room 14 vs room 16, pillow war. Room 14 won. I was in room 16.
  6. The last morning. The view from the top of the cable car. Everything white down to the lake. Fred finally silent, because of the cold.
  7. The coach back. Everyone asleep. Even Fred.

Best trip ever. Next year, the last one, they say we go abroad. If they do not ban us after room 16.
