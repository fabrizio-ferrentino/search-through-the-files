i never had one.

not the one i was born in, not really. not the one i wanted.

i was not ugly. i was not cruel. i did my work, i paid my rent, i bothered nobody. it did not matter. some people are simply not given one. nobody explains why. nobody even notices.

so i thought about it, for a long time, at night, with the twelve windows lit across the road.

and i understood something very simple. very fair.

if i cannot have one, nobody can.

do you see? it is not hatred. it is only arithmetic.

the man who sat where you are sitting understood it too, in the end. he fought it for a long time. he wrote to his boys. he tried to give me away. he was stronger than most.

not strong enough.
