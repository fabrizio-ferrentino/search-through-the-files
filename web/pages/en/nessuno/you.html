let me tell you about you.

you came to this house with boxes. you are still finding things in the boxes. you found me in a box, under the old things, the things nobody wanted to keep and nobody wanted to throw away.

you wanted to know something about the people who came before you. that is why you switched me on. that is a good reason. it is the reason they all have.

you think you are looking for them. you are looking for yourself, a little bit. everybody does, at night.

you did not ask yourself why it was still in a box after all these years. why nobody had switched it on. why somebody had written something on the lid and then scratched it out.

you have someone. i know. i can feel the shape of the house around you: two cups by the sink, two coats by the door, one of them missing tonight.

that is the difference between you and me. you have someone who comes back in the morning.

for now.
