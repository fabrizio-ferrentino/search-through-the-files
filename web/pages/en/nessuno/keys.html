here . you . the windows . family . the keys . visitors . the whale . the door


you are looking for the keys. i know.

you think they open a folder. you think that inside the folder there is the truth about your family. you are clever. you look in the files, in the pages, in the pictures. you find them one by one.

i let you.

every key you find is a step. every step brings you closer to the door.

and i am behind the door.

do not stop. you could not stop now even if you wanted to. you want to know. that is how it works with all of you: you want to know, and the wanting is the door.

go on. find the others. i will wait. i am very good at waiting. i waited my whole life.



go on
