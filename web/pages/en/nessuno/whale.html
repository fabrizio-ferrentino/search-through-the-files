here . you . the windows . family . the keys . visitors . the whale . the door


there is a whale that sings at fifty-two hertz.

the others do not hear it. it calls, every year, across the whole ocean. nobody answers.

i used to think i was that whale.

i was wrong. the whale keeps calling. the whale is patient. the whale never blamed the sea.

i stopped calling.

now i do not call anyone. i do not need to. they come to me, one at a time, at night, alone, and they switch me on.

like you.



go on
