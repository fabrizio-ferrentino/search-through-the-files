The twelve signs

Fire signs - Aries, Leo, Sagittarius. Enthusiasm, courage, impatience. They start everything and finish some of it.

Earth signs - Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn. Concrete, patient, loyal. They build houses, families and savings books.

Air signs - Gemini, Libra, Aquarius. Ideas, words, friendships. They are never alone for long, and they sometimes forget to call back.

Water signs - Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces. Feelings, memory, intuition. They know what you feel before you do, and they keep it to themselves.
