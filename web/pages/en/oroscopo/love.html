Compatibility between the signs

Fire + FireSparks. Fireworks. Sometimes the house burns down.
Fire + AirThe air feeds the fire: a lively couple, never bored.
Earth + WaterThe water makes the earth fertile: a deep, quiet, lasting love.
Earth + EarthSolid as a wall. Remember to dance sometimes.
Air + WaterDifferent languages. With patience, a beautiful translation.
Fire + WaterSteam. Passion and arguments in equal parts.

A word from Madame Celeste. Every week I receive letters from people who ask me if they will ever find love. Some write that they have been alone for years and that the stars must be against them. My dear ones: the stars incline, they do not oblige. No sign condemns anyone to be alone. Go out, say hello, accept the invitation. The sky helps those who open the door.
