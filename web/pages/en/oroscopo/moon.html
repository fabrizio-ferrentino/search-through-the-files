The moon in April 1999

Full moonWednesday 03/31/1999: feelings at the surface. Do not make big decisions at night.
Last quarteraround 04/08/1999: time to let go of what you no longer need.
New moonaround 04/16/1999: plant seeds, in the garden and in your life.
First quarteraround 04/22/1999: the moment to act.

The moon has always been the companion of those who are awake at night: sailors, nurses, bakers, mothers with newborn babies, and those who cannot sleep. The old women of the countryside said that you should never look at the full moon alone for too long, "or it starts to look back". It is only a saying. But on full moon nights, Madame Celeste draws the curtains.
