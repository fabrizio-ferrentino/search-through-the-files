The week sign by sign - from 03/29/1999 to 04/04/1999

The thought of the week: the stars incline, they do not oblige. Look up, then act.

The sky this week: from 03/29/1999 to 04/04/1999. The Sun has just entered Aries: the astrological year begins again. Full moon on Wednesday night: emotions run high, sleep runs short. Be careful with the decisions you make after midnight.

Aries 03/21 - 04/20 A week of energy. Use it for something you have been putting off: the car, the dentist, that phone call. Venus smiles on Thursday.
Taurus 04/21 - 05/20 Money matters ask for patience. Do not sign anything on Wednesday. At the weekend, a good meal with old friends.
Gemini 05/21 - 06/21 You talk a lot, you listen less. This week, try the other way round. Someone close has something to tell you.
Cancer 06/22 - 07/22 The house calls you: tidy, repaint, cook. Your family needs you more than you think, and you need them.
Leo 07/23 - 08/23 You will be noticed. Make sure it is for the right reason. Love: a surprise on Saturday.
Virgo 08/24 - 09/22 You want everything perfect. It will not be. It will be good, and that is enough.
Libra 09/23 - 10/22 A decision you have been avoiding comes back. Weigh it, then decide. The stars cannot do it for you.
Scorpio 10/23 - 11/22 Intense days. Do not stay up too late: the nights this week are not good counsellors. Sleep, and decide in the morning.
Sagittarius 11/23 - 12/21 A journey, maybe short, maybe only to a friend's house. Go. You will come back lighter.
Capricorn 12/22 - 01/20 Work, work, work. Remember that someone is waiting for you at home. Go home on time at least once.
Aquarius 01/21 - 02/19 New ideas, new people. Someone you meet by chance will matter. Say yes to an invitation.
Pisces 02/20 - 03/20 You feel everything twice as much this week. Be gentle with yourself. And with the people who are gentle with you.

The moon in April 1999

The moon changes face every night, and with it the mood of the house. Here are the phases that matter this month, to keep near the calendar in the kitchen.

Full moon Wednesday 03/31/1999: feelings at the surface. Do not make big decisions at night.
Last quarter around 04/08/1999: time to let go of what you no longer need.
New moon around 04/16/1999: plant seeds, in the garden and in your life.
First quarter around 04/22/1999: the moment to act.

The moon has always been the companion of those who are awake at night: sailors, nurses, bakers, mothers with newborn babies, and those who cannot sleep. The old women of the countryside said that you should never look at the full moon alone for too long, "or it starts to look back". It is only a saying. But on full moon nights, Madame Celeste draws the curtains.

Compatibility between the signs

Fire, Earth, Air and Water: every sign belongs to one of the four elements, and the elements get along, or do not, as people do. Find your two elements below.

Fire + Fire Sparks. Fireworks. Sometimes the house burns down.
Fire + Air The air feeds the fire: a lively couple, never bored.
Earth + Water The water makes the earth fertile: a deep, quiet, lasting love.
Earth + Earth Solid as a wall. Remember to dance sometimes.
Air + Water Different languages. With patience, a beautiful translation.
Fire + Water Steam. Passion and arguments in equal parts.

A word from Madame Celeste. Every week I receive letters from people who ask me if they will ever find love. Some write that they have been alone for years and that the stars must be against them. My dear ones: the stars incline, they do not oblige. No sign condemns anyone to be alone. Go out, say hello, accept the invitation. The sky helps those who open the door.

Ask Madame Celeste

Madame Celeste answers one letter a week on this page. Here are the latest ones, as they arrived, with her replies in gold.

Lucy, Pisces
My boyfriend is Scorpio. My mother says it will never work. Is she right?

Dear Lucy, water and water: you will understand each other without speaking. Your mother is a Virgo, I bet. Celeste

A Capricorn who works too much
I am never at home. My children are growing up without me. What do the stars say?

The stars say what you already know. Go home. Celeste

Anonymous, Aquarius
I am twenty-four and I have never had a girlfriend. Every week I read that I will meet someone. I never do. Are the stars lying to me?

My dear, the stars do not lie, but they do not knock on doors for you either. Write to me again, with your name this time, and tell me what you like to do. We will start from there. Celeste

Mr G., Scorpio
I cannot sleep. I wake up at three every night and I cannot go back to sleep. Is it the moon?

Dear Mr G., the moon can disturb sleep, but not every night. Talk to your doctor, and talk to your wife. Do not stay alone with your thoughts at three in the morning: they are never good company at that hour. Celeste
