The week sign by sign - from 03/29/1999 to 04/04/1999

Aries 03/21 - 04/20
A week of energy. Use it for something you have been putting off: the car, the dentist, that phone call. Venus smiles on Thursday.
Taurus 04/21 - 05/20
Money matters ask for patience. Do not sign anything on Wednesday. At the weekend, a good meal with old friends.
Gemini 05/21 - 06/21
You talk a lot, you listen less. This week, try the other way round. Someone close has something to tell you.
Cancer 06/22 - 07/22
The house calls you: tidy, repaint, cook. Your family needs you more than you think, and you need them.
Leo 07/23 - 08/23
You will be noticed. Make sure it is for the right reason. Love: a surprise on Saturday.
Virgo 08/24 - 09/22
You want everything perfect. It will not be. It will be good, and that is enough.
Libra 09/23 - 10/22
A decision you have been avoiding comes back. Weigh it, then decide. The stars cannot do it for you.
Scorpio 10/23 - 11/22
Intense days. Do not stay up too late: the nights this week are not good counsellors. Sleep, and decide in the morning.
Sagittarius 11/23 - 12/21
A journey, maybe short, maybe only to a friend's house. Go. You will come back lighter.
Capricorn 12/22 - 01/20
Work, work, work. Remember that someone is waiting for you at home. Go home on time at least once.
Aquarius 01/21 - 02/19
New ideas, new people. Someone you meet by chance will matter. Say yes to an invitation.
Pisces 02/20 - 03/20
You feel everything twice as much this week. Be gentle with yourself. And with the people who are gentle with you.
