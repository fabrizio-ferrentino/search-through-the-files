The week sign by sign - from 03/29/1999 to 04/04/1999

The thought of the week: the stars incline, they do not oblige. Look up, then act.

The sky this week: from 03/29/1999 to 04/04/1999. The Sun has just entered Aries: the astrological year begins again. Full moon on Wednesday night: emotions run high, sleep runs short. Be careful with the decisions you make after midnight.

Capricorn12/22 - 01/20Work, work, work. Remember that someone is waiting for you at home. Go home on time at least once.
Aquarius01/21 - 02/19New ideas, new people. Someone you meet by chance will matter. Say yes to an invitation.
Pisces02/20 - 03/20You feel everything twice as much this week. Be gentle with yourself. And with the people who are gentle with you.
