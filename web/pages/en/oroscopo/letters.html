Ask Madame Celeste

Madame Celeste answers one letter a week on this page. Here are the latest ones, as they arrived, with her replies in gold.

Lucy, Pisces
My boyfriend is Scorpio. My mother says it will never work. Is she right?

Dear Lucy, water and water: you will understand each other without speaking. Your mother is a Virgo, I bet. Celeste

A Capricorn who works too much
I am never at home. My children are growing up without me. What do the stars say?

The stars say what you already know. Go home. Celeste

Anonymous, Aquarius
I am twenty-four and I have never had a girlfriend. Every week I read that I will meet someone. I never do. Are the stars lying to me?

My dear, the stars do not lie, but they do not knock on doors for you either. Write to me again, with your name this time, and tell me what you like to do. We will start from there. Celeste

Mr G., Scorpio
I cannot sleep. I wake up at three every night and I cannot go back to sleep. Is it the moon?

Dear Mr G., the moon can disturb sleep, but not every night. Talk to your doctor, and talk to your wife. Do not stay alone with your thoughts at three in the morning: they are never good company at that hour. Celeste
