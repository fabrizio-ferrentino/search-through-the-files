My friends' sites and the pages I always visit. If a link does not work, it is because the site closed down. Lots of them close down. I leave them here anyway.

danny's page
My desk mate from school. He made a page about motorbikes, with photos of his. We used to write to each other every evening. Then he moved up north for work. The site has been gone for a while. So has he, I think.
S. and A.'s wedding
They made a website for their wedding, with the gift list and directions to the church. There was a photo page too. I am in one of them, bottom right, half cut off. They took it down after the honeymoon.
the lonely ring
A webring of personal pages of lonely people, like mine. From one page you went to the next, in a circle. I had joined. Every now and then I went through all of them, one after the other, and came back to mine. It felt less cold. It closed, I think for lack of members. Funny, if you think about it.
RetroForum
A message board about computers. I read the threads about modems. I never write: registration is closed, and anyway when I write, the thread dies. I like reading them. They help each other. They call each other by name.
the WebNet portal
Everything starts here. It is the first page I see when I connect. Every now and then I check whether there is anything new. There is always something new, and nothing for me.

If you have a page and you want me to put it here, tell me. I put your link and you, if you want, put mine. That is how it works, right? You swap.
