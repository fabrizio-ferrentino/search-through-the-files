thoughts
« back to the room · i write them when i get back from my shift. the newest is at the bottom.

~ one night in october ~
The page is finished! The counter works. I asked my cousin to try it and he said "nice". He was visitor number 2. Number 1 was me.

~ one night in november ~
Tonight in the chat room I said hello three times. "Hi everyone." Once somebody answered, but it was the automatic message of the room that lists the rules. The others were talking among themselves about a party. I just sat and read. You can be in a room full of people on the Internet too, I found out. It is like the table at weddings.

~ one night in december ~
I put an ad in the lonely hearts column. It took me two hours to write. I deleted "I am shy" because a magazine said women want confident men. I deleted "I work nights" because it sounds sad. In the end what was left was "looking for someone to talk to". I left it like that. We will see.

~ christmas night ~
Working tonight too, I asked for it. In the warehouse the radio was on. At midnight they wished merry christmas "to all the families listening". I switched the radio off. The twelve windows across the street were all lit tonight. All of them.

~ one night in january ~
No answer to the ad. I put it up again, the same. A colleague told me I do everything wrong, that I have to be more decisive, that women want a man who knows what he wants. He lent me a leaflet for a "method" you buy by mail order. It promised that in thirty days they would be queueing up. In the photos everybody was tanned and smiling. I am not tanned. I don't know if I smile.

~ one night in february ~
I asked a girl at the bar if she would like a coffee some time, outside the bar. She laughed. It was not a mean laugh, and that was worse. She said "oh come on" as if I had made a joke. I laughed too, so it would really look like a joke. I am good at pretending it was a joke.

~ one night in march ~
I think about it a lot: it is not that I am ugly. It is not that I am bad. I do my job, I pay the rent, I don't bother anybody. It is that they don't see me. I am like the station behind my building: still there, but the train does not stop. Everybody tells me what I should change. Nobody asks me how I am. Men don't ask each other how they are, it seems. They slap each other on the back and talk about football.

~ one night in april ~
The counter is at 17. Seventeen in all, since october. Ten of them are me checking that it works. I removed the background music from the page, nobody was hearing it anyway.

~ one night, i don't know which anymore ~
The twelve windows. I watch them every evening. One family per window. The little boy on the second floor does his homework at the kitchen table and his mother runs her hand through his hair. She does not do it on purpose, she just does it, while she is thinking of something else. I never had that hand, not even as a child.

I know it is not their fault. I know. They never did anything to me. But tonight, for the first time, I thought something ugly looking at that window, and I can't get it out of my head. I won't write it. I won't write it.

end of the thoughts. the rest is in the update.
