ABSTRACT
--------

These pages collect two years of data on how men and women choose each other in the last part of this century. I am not a scientist and I do not pretend to be one. I am a man who got tired of being told that it was his fault and decided to count instead.

The results are presented without comment wherever possible. Where comment is necessary I keep it short. Those who disagree are invited to bring numbers. Opinions will not be answered.

Summary of the findings, for those who will not read the rest (most of you):

1. A small minority of men receives the large majority of female attention. (see the 80/20)
2. Women choose upwards, consistently, across every income and age group. (see hypergamy)
3. The value of a man and the value of a woman follow opposite curves with age. (see age and value)
4. Of 112 men surveyed on the board, 61 have never had a relationship. (see the survey)

A fifth section, field notes, contains the raw observations on which part of the above is based. It is the least interesting page for the general reader and the most important for me.
