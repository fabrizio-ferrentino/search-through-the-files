THE SURVEY
----------

In 03/1998 I asked the members of the board to answer eight questions by e-mail. 112 answered. Some did it in ten minutes. One wrote eleven pages. The answers are anonymous; the percentages are mine.

never had a relationship..###################################################### 61 of 112
never kissed..............######################################### 46
lives with parents........#################################### 40
has a close friend........##################### 24
would talk to a doctor....######## 9
thinks it will change.....#### 5

Question 8 was open: "what would you need?". The most common answer, in various words, was "to be chosen once". The second most common was "nothing, it is too late". The third was a blank line. I counted the blank lines too. There were nineteen.

One answer I keep re-reading, from a member who has not posted since: "I would need someone to ask me how I am without wanting to fix me." I do not know how to put that in a chart, so I put it here.
