AGE AND VALUE
-------------

This is the page that gets me the most letters of insult, so I will be brief. Value here means only one thing: the number of replies a lonely hearts ad receives, by age of the person who wrote it. Same papers as the 80/20.

men, 20-29....###### 6 replies on average
men, 30-39....######### 9
men, 40-49....####### 7
women, 20-29..############################### 31
women, 30-39..################## 18
women, 40-49..###### 6

Women begin rich and become poor. Men begin poor and, if they have money and patience, become a little less poor. The two lines cross somewhere around forty. The men on the board call that point the wall. I do not like the word. I like the numbers even less.

Note for the young readers who write to me in despair: the curve says that time works for you. Note for the old readers: the curve says that time worked for somebody.
