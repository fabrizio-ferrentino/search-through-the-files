HYPERGAMY
---------

Definition used here: the tendency to choose a partner of higher status than oneself. Not a moral judgement. A direction, like water going down.

Source: the wedding announcements of one regional newspaper for the whole of 1996, 312 couples, where the profession of both spouses is given. I ranked the professions by average income using the published tables.

he earns more...#################################### 71% of couples
about the same..########### 22%
she earns more..#### 7%

The 7% are interesting. I telephoned eleven of them. Three agreed to talk to me. In all three cases the husband was, in her own words, "very good looking". Status moves; the direction does not.

What follows from this for a man like me, or like the readers of the board, is left to the reader. I will say only this: every system where the water goes down has a bottom. Somebody lives there.
