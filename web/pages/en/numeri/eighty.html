THE 80/20
---------

Source: 400 lonely hearts ads published by women in four local papers between 01/1997 and 12/1997, compared with the replies the papers say they forwarded. I called the papers pretending to be a sociology student. Two of them believed me.

top 20% of men..######################################## 79% of replies accepted
next 30%........######## 15%
next 30%........## 5%
bottom 20%...... 1%

The shape of the chart is the whole argument. Men are not chosen on a curve. They are chosen in a corner.

Objection I receive most often: "the ads are not reality". Correct. The ads are what women write when nobody is watching them choose. That is closer to reality than what they say at dinner.

Note: the bottom 20% includes the men who did not reply at all because they already knew. I estimated them. It is the only estimated number on this page, and I estimated it upwards, to be kind.
