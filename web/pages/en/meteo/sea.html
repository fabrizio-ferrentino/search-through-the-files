Avalanche risk is measured on a scale from 1 to 5. At 3 the snow is unstable on many slopes: stay on the marked runs and do not go off-piste alone. Spring snow is the most treacherous: firm in the morning, heavy and wet in the afternoon, when most accidents happen.

For sailors. The sea state is given on the Douglas scale: 3 is slight, 4 moderate, 5 rough. A sea that looks calm from the beach can be very different a mile out: listen to the marine forecast on the radio before you go out, and tell someone ashore when you expect to be back.
