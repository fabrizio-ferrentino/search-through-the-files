FORECAST FOR WEDNESDAY 03/31/1999
issued at 6:00 AM · next update at 6:00 PM

General situation. A cold front coming from the north-west crosses the country during the day. Cloudy skies in the north with showers in the afternoon, snow above 1,200 metres. Sunny spells in the centre and the south, but with a cold wind. Temperatures falling everywhere, below the seasonal average.
