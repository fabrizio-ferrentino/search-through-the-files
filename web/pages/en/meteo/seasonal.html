The season ahead: April, May, June 1999

Seasonal forecasts are not forecasts in the usual sense: nobody can tell you whether it will rain on the fifteenth of May. They say whether the season will be, on average, warmer or colder, wetter or drier than usual. Take them for what they are: a tendency.

Last year. Our seasonal forecast for the summer of 1998 said "hot and dry". It was hot and dry. Our forecast for the autumn said "mild". It snowed in November. We report both, because a site that only tells you when it was right is not a site to trust.

The gardener's calendar. Plant tomatoes outside only after the middle of April, when the nights stay above ten degrees. Courgettes and beans can wait for May. The old saying is right more often than we are: wait until the late frosts are over before you put away the winter coats - and the plants' covers.
