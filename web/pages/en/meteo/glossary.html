The words of the weather

  • Anticyclone - an area of high pressure. It brings stable, dry weather: sun in summer, fog and cold in winter.
  • Depression - an area of low pressure, the opposite. Clouds, wind, rain. Also what readers feel after a week of it.
  • Cold front - the edge of a mass of cold air that pushes under warm air. Short, violent showers, then clear skies and a drop in temperature.
  • Warm front - the edge of warm air sliding over cold air. Long, steady rain, grey skies, a slow rise in temperature.
  • Isobars - the lines on the map that join points with the same pressure. The closer they are, the stronger the wind.
  • Dew point - the temperature at which the air can no longer hold its water. When the night falls below it, there is dew on the grass, or fog.
  • Foehn - a dry, warm wind that comes down the mountains. It melts the snow in a day and, the old people say, gives everyone a headache.
  • Precipitation - anything that falls from the sky: rain, snow, sleet, hail. Not frogs. We have been asked.
  • Probability of rain - "40%" does not mean it will rain for 40% of the day. It means that on ten days like this one, it rains on four.

Is a word missing? Write to us: the most requested ones are added at the end of every month. The last one added, after many letters, was "Foehn", from readers who live in the valleys and were tired of explaining it to their relatives in the city.
