Rules

  1. Ads are free and stay online for three months.
  2. Only private sellers. Shops have their own sites.
  3. Describe the computer honestly: model, age, defects. Defects you hide come out on the first day.
  4. No stolen goods. If a price is too low to be true, it is not true.
  5. Meet in daylight, in a public place or at the seller's home with someone else present.
  6. The editors do not guarantee anything, do not handle money and do not take part in the deals.

We are three enthusiasts with a lot of old computers in our cellars. Be patient with us.
