Issue of 02/03/1999 · page 11

Night-time ram raid at supermarket, thieves take the safe
In and out in under ten minutes, using a stolen van as a battering ram. Police studying the security camera footage.

They smashed through the glass front with a van stolen a few hours earlier, went in, loaded the safe onto a trolley and drove off. All in less than ten minutes. That is the story of last night's ram raid at the supermarket on the north side of town, the third raid of its kind in the county since the start of the year.

The alarm went off at 2:40 AM, but by the time a patrol car arrived the thieves were long gone. The van, reported stolen the evening before in a nearby town, was left at the entrance with its engine still running. The raiders, at least three according to first reports, are thought to have escaped in a second car waiting in the car park.

The safe held the weekend's takings, a sum the manager would not disclose. The damage to the building is also considerable: the glass front was completely destroyed and the supermarket stayed closed until noon so that it could be made safe.

Police are going through the recordings of the security cameras, which are said to have caught at least one of the thieves with his face uncovered. "We are asking anyone who noticed anything unusual in the area to come forward", the station commander appealed. "Even a detail that seems unimportant can help".
