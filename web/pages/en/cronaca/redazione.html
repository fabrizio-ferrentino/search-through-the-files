The County Herald has come out every morning since 1952, Sundays included. It is written by a newsroom of fourteen journalists and about thirty correspondents spread around the towns of the county, who tell us about fêtes, council meetings, Sunday matches and, sadly, misfortunes too.

Tips. Got a story, a photo, a complaint? Write to us or drop by the newsroom: we are open to the public Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 1 PM. Letters to the editor are printed on Thursdays.

Corrections. If an article in the archive contains mistakes, or if you were involved in a news story and would like your name removed from the online version, write to the newsroom giving the headline and the date. We will consider every request.
