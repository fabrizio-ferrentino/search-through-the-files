The damaged scans. Some microfilm reels from our old offices were ruined by damp. From those issues we managed to recover the text of a few articles, but not the dates: they appear in the archive as ??/??/19??. If you keep old copies of the Herald at home, you can help us date them by bringing them to the newsroom: we will give them back, promise.

Subscriptions. The online archive is free for the whole of 1999. From next year, articles older than one month will be reserved for subscribers to the paper edition, who will receive a personal password with their card.
