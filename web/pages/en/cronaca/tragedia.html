Night of horror in a quiet suburb: man kills his wife and attacks his son
The neighbour, who ran over at the screams, managed to stop him. The man died in the struggle. The boy is in hospital in a serious condition but is not believed to be in danger.

A night of terror shook the residential area on the edge of town yesterday, a neighbourhood of terraced houses lived in mostly by young families. Shortly after three in the morning a middle-aged man, an office worker with no criminal record, killed his wife in the bedroom of their home and then turned on their elder son, who had been woken by the noise.

The alarm was raised by the boy's screams, which neighbours heard through the open windows. The man next door, a retired widower, came in through the kitchen door, which had been left ajar, and found the father on the landing, standing over his son. A violent struggle followed, at the end of which the attacker fell down the stairs. The paramedics, who arrived a few minutes later, could only confirm his death.

The boy, who had several injuries, was taken to the county hospital and operated on during the night. Doctors are not yet giving a prognosis, but early reports say his life is not in danger. The couple had a second, younger son: investigators declined to make any statement about him.

"A normal family". Nobody in the neighbourhood can explain what happened. "They were a normal family, quiet, the kind that always says hello", says a neighbour. "He washed the car with the boys on Sundays, took them to the seaside in the summer. Never a fight, never a raised voice." Some, however, remember that the man had changed in recent months. "He had lost weight, his eyes looked tired", says the owner of the café on the corner. "He told me he wasn't sleeping anymore. At night, walking past, you could always see a window lit upstairs, the study. The blue light of the computer, until dawn."

The man had no record and no known health problems, and does not appear to have been seeing any doctor. Investigators are saying nothing about a motive. The house has been sealed off to allow forensic officers to work, which they did until late in the evening.

To protect the child, the Herald is not publishing the names of the people involved or the address of the house.
