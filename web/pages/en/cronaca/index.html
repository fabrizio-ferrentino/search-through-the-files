Welcome to the Herald's online archive. Since this year the articles printed in the paper are being moved onto the Internet, one year at a time, starting from the most recent. It is slow work: every page is scanned and then typed in again by hand by our staff. We thank our readers for their patience.

Note to readers: on some pages the scan of the old issues is damaged. Where the date cannot be read it is shown as ??/??/19??. We are collecting the paper copies from the county library to correct them.
