Certificates and documents

Online certificate requests are not active. The regional project to issue certificates through the Internet has been postponed. For now, certificates are requested at the registry office, in person.

Residence and family certificates are issued on the spot. Bring an identity document. If you request a certificate for someone else, you need their signed authorisation.

The identity card is valid for five years. Bring three passport photographs, the old card and, if you have lost it, the police report.

Births are registered within ten days, at the hospital or at the town hall. Marriages: the banns are posted for eight days on the notice board outside the town hall, which is still the most read page in town.

Changes of residence: tell us within twenty days. The municipal police will come to check that you really live there - it is the law, not curiosity.
