Offices and opening hours

The offices of the town hall and their hours:

  • Registry office (births, residence, identity cards): Mon-Sat 8:30 AM - 12:30 PM, Thu also 3 PM - 5:30 PM
  • Technical office (building permits, roads): Tue and Thu 9 AM - 12 PM
  • Social services: Mon, Wed, Fri 9 AM - 12 PM, by appointment
  • Tax office: Mon-Fri 9 AM - 12 PM
  • Agriculture office: Wed 9 AM - 12 PM
  • Municipal police: every day 8 AM - 8 PM
  • The mayor: Saturday morning, by appointment

Social services also help people who live alone and need company or a hand with shopping: the volunteers of the parish and of the pensioners' association visit them once a week. If you know someone who needs it, tell us. If it is you, tell us anyway: it is not a shame.

Offices are closed on public holidays and on the feast of the patron saint.
