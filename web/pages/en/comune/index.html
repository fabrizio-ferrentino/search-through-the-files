Welcome to the Town of Millbrook

Millbrook, 6,400 inhabitants, a lake, a river, three schools, a library, a football ground and a town hall that has been "temporarily" in the old school building since 1971. This site is the town hall's window on the Internet: here you find opening hours, notices, events and the news of the council.
