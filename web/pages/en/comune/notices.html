Notices

The notices of the council, the most recent first. The paper copies stay on the notice board outside the town hall.

  • 03/30/1999 - Road works on Lake Road. From 04/06/1999 to 04/23/1999, between the bridge and the campsite. Diversion through Church Street. We apologise for the inconvenience: the road had more holes than asphalt.
  • 03/26/1999 - Easter procession. Good Friday from 8:30 PM. Parking forbidden on the route from 6 PM.
  • 03/22/1999 - Late frost. Damage reports at the agriculture office by 04/15/1999.
  • 03/15/1999 - Dogs in the park. Dogs must be kept on a lead in the lakeside park. The owners know who they are.
  • 03/08/1999 - Census of trees. The council is counting the trees along the town's streets. If you see a person with a tape measure hugging a plane tree, do not worry.
  • 02/26/1999 - New telephone numbers. From April the area code must be dialled for local calls too. The town hall's numbers do not change otherwise.
  • 02/10/1999 - Street lights. The street lights of the new housing estate on the edge of town will be repaired by the end of March. Residents are asked to report the lamps that still do not work.

Last updated: 03/30/1999 - Town Hall data office
