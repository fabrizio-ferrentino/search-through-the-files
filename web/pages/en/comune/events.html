Events in town

The calendar of the spring events, as far as we know it today.

  • Good Friday 04/02/1999 - The Easter procession, from the church to the lake and back, by candlelight.
  • Easter Monday 04/05/1999 - Picnic in the lakeside park, music from the town band from 3 PM.
  • Saturday 04/17/1999 - Spring market in the square: plants, seeds, honey and the cakes of the parish ladies.
  • Sunday 05/02/1999 - "Clean the lake": volunteers wanted, gloves and sandwiches provided by the council.
  • Saturday 05/15/1999 - Local history afternoon at the library.
  • June 1999 - The patron saint's festival: three days of stalls, the funfair, and the fireworks over the lake.

Associations that want to put an event in the calendar can write to the culture office by the fifteenth of the month before.
