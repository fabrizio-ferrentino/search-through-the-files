Events in town

Good Friday 04/02/1999
The Easter procession, from the church to the lake and back, by candlelight.

Picnic in the lakeside park, music from the town band from 3 PM.

Spring market in the square: plants, seeds, honey and the cakes of the parish ladies.

"Clean the lake": volunteers wanted, gloves and sandwiches provided by the council.

Local history afternoon at the library.

The patron saint's festival: three days of stalls, the funfair, and the fireworks over the lake.
