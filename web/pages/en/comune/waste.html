Mondaypaper and cardboard
Tuesdaygeneral waste
Wednesdayglass and cans
Thursdayplastic
Fridaygeneral waste
Saturdaygarden waste (from April to October)

Put the bags out after 8 PM the evening before, not before: the cats of the town are organised, and they know the timetable better than you.

Large items (furniture, old appliances, computers): call the technical office to book a free collection. Do not leave them next to the bins. Last month someone left a whole computer, screen and keyboard, next to the bins in Lake Road. It was gone the next morning before the lorry arrived. We do not know who took it.

Batteries and medicines go in the special containers at the chemist's and at the school.
