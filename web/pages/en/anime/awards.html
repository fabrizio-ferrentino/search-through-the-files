Awards
Thank you so much to everyone who thought this little shrine was worth a prize! The award pictures are small because my page was getting slow. I am only allowed to link inside this site, so the sites that gave the awards are written as text.

The Golden Star Award, given by The Anime Attic on 02/14/1999. "A sweet and well organised shrine with lots of love for its series. Very nice colours; please make the font a bit bigger." I made it bigger. Thanks!

The Cutest Page Award, from Moonpie's Fan Corner, 02/28/1999. I won it by a vote of seven to six, and I am told one of the six was Moonpie's brother.

Pixie Pilot Fan Club Member of the Month, March 1999. It was the fourth month in a row that the webmaster of the Pip Fan Club gave it to himself, but he gave it to me anyway, which was nice.

Bronze Ribbon for Best Episode Guide, Cartoon Guide Network, 03/10/1999. "Complete and correct, except for episode 3, which is actually the second episode in some areas."

Honourable Mention - Sparkle Prize, "for the most blinking stars on a single page". (That was not about this page, they said. They meant the guestbook.)

How I give awards. If you have a Pixie Pilot Hikari site with at least five pages, a fan-made theme song, or drawings that are your own, write me in the guestbook. I give out two awards a month, no more, because I have to do the maths homework too.

Rules for winners: put the picture on your main page, keep it small, and tell me when you move. Also: do not steal pictures from other shrines. It is not nice.

Awards page last changed 03/28/1999.
