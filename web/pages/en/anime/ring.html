The Pixie Pilot Ring
A web ring is a circle of sites about the same thing: you click Next and you go to the next shrine, until you come back to the first. This one has 31 members, as of 03/26/1999. It was started in January by the webmaster of the Moonpie Fan Corner.

This Pixie Pilot Hikari site belongs to the Pixie Pilot Ring, and it is member number 17.

Note. Ring pages sometimes load slowly; the ring server is a school computer in another town and it is switched off at night. If the Next button says "cannot find server", try again in the afternoon.

Other shrines I like (written as text because I am a polite guest, not because they are bad)
  • Moonpie's Fan Corner (www.webnet.com/~moonpie) - lots of wallpapers and a good page about Pip
  • Hikari Fan Two (www.webnet.com/~hikari_fan2) - a very serious site with a timeline of the series
  • The Anime Attic - an archive of cartoon theme songs in MIDI
  • Cartoon Guide Network - TV timetables for Saturday morning
Want to join the ring? Your site must be about Pixie Pilot Hikari, have at least three pages and not too many pictures on the main page, because the ring page has to load on a 28.8 modem. You must add the ring code to your main page. The code is long, so I cannot print it here: ask in the guestbook and I will send it to you.

Banners. If you want to swap banners with me, mine is 468 by 60 pixels with Hikari on the left and the ship on the right. Please do not put it on a page with music, because it makes my site look like a juke box.
