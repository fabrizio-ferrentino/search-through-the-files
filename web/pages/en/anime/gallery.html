Fan art gallery
My scanner has been broken since Christmas, so for now the pictures are described in words. When Dad fixes it (or when the one at school is free) I will scan them all. Meanwhile, here is what you sent me. You can send drawings to the school computer room, to my name, or ask me for the postal address in the guestbook. No pictures of the characters in a bad light, please.

1. Tamara, 13, Millbrook
Hikari on the roof of the greenhouse, in pencil, with Primrose in the corner. She has coloured the sky with a dark blue felt pen and used white correction fluid for the stars.

2. Dario, 16, from the lake area
Sir Prickly reading his poem to a wall. Pen and ink, very neat. The wall has a speech bubble that says nothing.

3. Giulia and her little sister
A drawing in crayons of Pip eating a giant strawberry. The strawberry is bigger than the ship. The little sister did the leaves.

4. Stefan, 12
Captain Bellflower on a motorbike. This is not in the series, he says, but it should be. Cape, helmet, a very serious face.

5. Miki (me)
The Moon Rabbit with her cup of tea. Watercolour. It took four evenings and the tea in the picture looks a bit like soup.

6. Anonymous, from the library bulletin board
A cut-out paper Hikari taped to the window of the children's room of the library. Somebody added a paper Pip a week later.

How to send fan art: drawings must be your own. Say your name, your age and your town. I will put it here with your first name only.
