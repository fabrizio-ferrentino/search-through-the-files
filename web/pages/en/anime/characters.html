Characters
Here are the main characters of the first block of Pixie Pilot Hikari. Birthdays are from the official booklet that came with the sticker album, which I bought at the stationery shop on Lake Road. Ages are the ones given in the show.

Hikari Tsukino
pilot of the Blossom Fleet · age: 11 · birthday: May 5
Clumsy, loyal and always hungry. Her ship is called Primrose. Favourite food: rice balls with plum. She cannot say no to anybody who asks for help, which is her great strength and the reason she is always late.

Pip
pixie, Hikari's co-pilot · age: 3 · birthday: April 1
Three inches tall, talks too fast, hates Mondays and loves jam. She knows the star charts by heart and invents the plans that Hikari then ruins. Voice: high and scratchy.

Captain Bellflower
captain of the Blossom Fleet · age: 27 · birthday: October 12
Tall, calm, wears a long violet cape even in the bath (episode 8). She speaks little and when she does it is usually a proverb about gardens. Fans have written 200 letters asking about her past. I am still waiting.

Sir Prickly
knight of the Gloom Court · age: unknown · birthday: never said
A hedgehog in a black suit of armour who writes terrible poetry. He is not really wicked, only sad and misunderstood, which makes him the best villain on television. Favourite food: cold soup.

Moon Rabbit
keeper of the tea · age: very old · birthday: full moon
Appears at the end of the first block. Very polite, never hurries, drinks tea from a cup with no bottom. Rumour: she is the one who sent the flower to Hikari's grandmother's greenhouse.

Grandma Tsukino
keeper of the greenhouse · age: 71 · birthday: February 2
The most relaxed person in the cartoon. Grows tomatoes the size of fists, has never been surprised by a spaceship. Her one line in every episode is 'tea is ready'.
