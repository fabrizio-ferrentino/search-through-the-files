Episode guide
Pixie Pilot Hikari has 26 episodes in all, and Channel 9 has shown 12 of them. A new one comes every Saturday at 8:30 AM and is repeated on Wednesday at 4:00 PM, so the dates below are the first broadcast in our area. I copied the titles from the TV listings; the summaries are mine. Episodes 13 to 26 are coming, and I am already nervous.

Episode 1 - Hikari and the Flower in the Greenhouse. Hikari goes to water her grandmother's tomatoes and finds a flower that hums. It opens, and inside there is a ship the size of a teacup. A pixie called Pip falls out of the cockpit and says, in a very small voice, that she is late for work. Best moment: the ship grows to full size and takes the roof off the greenhouse; Hikari's grandmother just says "I will call the glazier".

Episode 5 - Captain Bellflower Comes to School. The captain of the Blossom Fleet disguises herself as a supply teacher to look for the stolen Garden Star, and nobody notices because she keeps her cape on. Everybody loved this one. The whole class ends up in the cargo bay, and the maths test is cancelled.

Episode 10 - Sir Prickly's Terrible Poem. The villain writes a poem so sad that every flower in town droops. Hikari fights it with the worst poem ever written, by herself. Fans are divided: I think it is the funniest episode, my cousin says it is the one with the best music.

Episode 12 - Hikari Meets the Moon Rabbit. The finale of the first block. A white rabbit who lives on the moon asks Hikari for a cup of tea and a favour. I will not tell you the favour. At the end, a new ship appears in the sky, and the credits roll over the first notes of a song nobody has heard yet. The wait for episode 13 is terrible.

Note: dates are the Channel 9 dates for Millbrook and the lake area. In other regions the show may be on a different day. If your dates are different, tell me in the guestbook.
