Hikari's Shrine
a fan site for Pixie Pilot Hikari
Welcome, fellow star pilots!
This is a shrine to Pixie Pilot Hikari, the best cartoon on Channel 9's Saturday morning block. I am Miki, I am fourteen, I live in Millbrook and I have taped every episode shown so far on a VHS that is now so worn that Hikari's hair has gone green. This site is for everybody who loves the show as much as I do.

What is Pixie Pilot Hikari? It is the story of Hikari Tsukino, a clumsy eleven-year-old who finds a tiny flower-shaped spaceship in her grandmother's greenhouse and discovers she is the new pilot of the Blossom Fleet, whose job is to guard the Garden Stars from the Gloom Court. Each episode she learns a new lesson, loses her homework, and saves a star. The theme song has been stuck in my head since January.

What you will find here
  • Episode guide - the episodes shown so far, with the dates of the first broadcast in our area
  • Characters - the pilots, the pixies and the villains, with their birthdays and favourite foods
  • Fan art gallery - drawings sent in by visitors, described because my scanner is broken
  • Awards - the little prizes this shrine has won (thank you!!)
  • Web ring - the Pixie Pilot Ring and other shrines
  • Guestbook - tell me what you think
