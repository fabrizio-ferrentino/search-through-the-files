Board rules
Last updated by the webmaster on 01/10/1999. By posting you agree to all of them. They are not many, and they are mostly common sense.

  1. Be polite. Everybody here was a beginner once. Insults, flame wars and "RTFM" without an explanation get a warning; the third warning gets you banned.
  2. Search before you ask. Your question has probably been answered already, possibly twelve times. Use the archive.
  3. Describe your problem. Write what you have (processor, RAM, operating system), what you did, and what the computer said. Copy the error message exactly.
  4. One topic, one thread. Do not post the same question in three sections. We will notice and we will move it, and we will sigh.
  5. No pirated software. No serial numbers, no cracks, no "who has the full version". This one has no warnings, only bans.
  6. No chain letters, no "forward this to ten people or else", no virus warnings you did not check. Most of them are fake, and the real ones are not that funny either.
  7. DO NOT WRITE IN CAPITALS. It is like shouting. Signatures: four lines at most, no pictures.
  8. The Marketplace is for members. Be honest in your ads. If something you sell has a problem, write it.
  9. No posting between midnight and 6 AM. The server runs its nightly backup and anything written during that time may be lost. Posts that appear during those hours anyway are removed without notice. Do not reply to them.
  10. The moderators have the last word. If you disagree, write to the webmaster, politely, during the day.

That is all. Have fun, help each other, and keep a backup of everything that matters to you.
— the webmaster
