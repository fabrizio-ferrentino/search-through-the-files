Classic. 56k only works if the line between you and the telephone exchange is clean, and most home lines are not. A few things to try, in order:

1. Download the newest drivers from the manufacturer's site, not the ones on the CD (they are always old).
2. Plug the modem directly into the wall socket, no extension cables, no splitters.
3. Anything else on the same line? Fax, answering machine, a second phone, an alarm system? Unplug them all and try again.
4. Pick up a phone while nobody is calling and listen. If you hear a hiss or a hum, call the phone company, not the provider.
