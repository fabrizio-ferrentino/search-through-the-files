it just makes that tick-tick-tick we all know
If the disk still spins, try DISKCOPY onto a fresh floppy: sometimes it skips the bad sectors and you get almost everything back. Do it ONCE, every try scratches it a little more. And whatever you do, do not say yes to "format".

Also: tell your sister to keep copies next time. On two floppies. In two different drawers.

Anna is right. If DISKCOPY gives up, there are disk repair utilities that read a sector over and over until they get it (or until they die trying). It can take an hour for a single floppy. Leave it running and go to bed.

Text files are the easiest to save: even if half the file is gone, you can open what is left with Notepad and copy it out. Word documents are harder, but the text is usually in there somewhere, between the garbage.
