Not a stupid question at all, Tom, this is the most asked question of the year. Short answer: a PC bought in 1998 is almost certainly fine. Planes are not my department.

If you want to test it yourself:
1. Save everything and close all programs.
2. Set the date to December 31st, 1999 and the time to 11:58 PM.
3. Wait three minutes, then restart the computer.
4. If after the restart the date says January 1st, 2000, your BIOS is fine.

Then put the date back to today, or your e-mails will arrive from the future.

Check the BIOS first, then the programs. In that order. The BIOS is the clock of the machine; if it is wrong, everything on top of it is wrong too. Older programs, especially spreadsheets and accounting software, are where the real trouble is. Most companies have free patches on their sites.
