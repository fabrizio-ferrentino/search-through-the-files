The surprise is at number one: Night Shift, a small adventure made by two brothers in their garage, has overtaken the game of the month in a week. We asked them why they think people like it. "Because it is about being alone in a building at night", they wrote. "Everyone has been there".

The chart counts the downloads from our archive between the first and the last day of the month. Downloads that stop halfway do not count - which, with the phone lines of this month, means about a third of them.
