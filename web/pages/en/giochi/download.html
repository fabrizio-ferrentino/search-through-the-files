Advice for downloading, for when we are back. Download at night, when the line is free and the call costs less. Never pick up the phone while a download is running: the modem hangs up and you start again. Big files are split into floppy-sized pieces: download them all, put them in the same folder, then run the first one.

Shareware, freeware, demo: what is the difference? Freeware is free, full stop. Shareware is free to try: if you like it, you send the author the money and receive the full version, usually by post, on floppies. A demo is a piece of a game sold in shops, to make you want the rest. Copying the full version of a shareware game is not clever: it is stealing from two guys in a garage.

Viruses. Everything in our archive was checked before going online. That does not mean you should not check it again: update your antivirus at least once a month, and be suspicious of anyone who sends you a game by e-mail that you did not ask for.
