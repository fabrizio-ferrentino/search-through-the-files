Cheats, codes and secrets

Type the codes during play unless we say otherwise. If a code does not work, check the version of the game: the cheats change between versions, and the authors close the ones that get too famous.

Dungeon of Ash
LANTERN  - full light in every room
IRONSKIN - you cannot be hurt
GOLDRUSH - 1000 gold coins
Sent by three readers. The second one says LANTERN "spoils the fun". The third one says the fun was already spoiled by the spiders.
Starhauler
at the station, hold SHIFT and type CARGOCULT: hold full of rare goods
Works only in the shareware version. The full version answers: "Nice try, captain".
Brick Fury
on the title screen: UP UP DOWN DOWN, then SPACE
You start from level 20. Do it only if you have already lost the will to live on level 19.
Tank Wars Deluxe
type BIGBANG while aiming
The next shot is a nuclear one. Your brother will not speak to you for a week.
Night Shift
No cheats. The authors say: "There is no code to turn the lights on. That is the game".
