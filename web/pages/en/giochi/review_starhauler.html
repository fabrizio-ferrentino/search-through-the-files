Starhauler
space trading · shareware episode 3.1 MB, full version by post · reviewed by the PlayWeb team

You start with a ship that costs less than its paint, a debt with a man you would rather not meet, and a map of eleven star systems. Buy cheap, sell dear, avoid the pirates, pay the debt. Simple. It is not.

The beauty of Starhauler is in the small things. The prices change because things happen: a famine on a planet makes wheat precious, a war two systems away makes everyone want metal. The pirates remember you. The station mechanic tells you jokes, and they get worse as your hull gets better. And there is the cat, who appears in the full version and does absolutely nothing useful, and whom you will defend with your life.

The defects: the graphics are from another decade, the sound is a few beeps, and the combat is clumsy - you will learn to run away rather than fight, which may be the point. The save system is merciless: one slot, overwritten automatically. Lose a cargo, and you live with it.

The verdict. We started playing it to write this review and we were still playing at three in the morning, a week later. That has not happened since we were fifteen.
