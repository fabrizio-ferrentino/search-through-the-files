Notice to customers

From April 1999 I no longer make evening or night calls. Only from 8 AM to 7 PM, Monday to Saturday. I apologise to those who counted on me after dinner.

Some of you have asked why. The honest answer is that I have read the newspaper, like everyone in town, and I had been in that house. I cannot say more, and I do not want to.

I heard that the police took the computer away and then gave it back to the family. If anyone in that family is reading this: do not keep it. Do not sell it. Do not give it to anyone. Bring it to me, in daylight, and I will take it to the tip myself, and I will not charge you anything.

To everyone else: switch your computers off at night. Go to bed. Talk to the people in your house. I have been inside two thousand homes, and the ones that were well were never the ones with the best computer.

- the doctor, 04/1999
