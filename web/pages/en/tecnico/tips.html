Tips from the doctor

Switch it off. A computer is not a fridge. Switch it off at night: it lasts longer, it costs less, and you sleep better. Many of my customers who sleep badly have a computer that never sleeps.

Make copies. On floppies, on the second disk, anywhere. The day the disk breaks is never a good day.

Do not open attachments from people you do not know. And from people you know, if you were not expecting them.

Second-hand computers. They can be a good deal. Have them checked, and have the disk formatted before you use them: you do not know what the previous owner left inside, and you do not want to find out by accident.

Keep it in a room where the family passes. The computer in a closed room, at night, alone: that is where I find the most viruses, the most strange programs and, sometimes, the most worried families.
