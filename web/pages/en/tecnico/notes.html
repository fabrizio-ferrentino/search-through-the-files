Job notes

Every now and then I publish here the notes of the most curious jobs, without names or addresses, because many problems repeat themselves and someone might recognise theirs. Most are funny. Not all.

JOB 1187 · 01/1999
Computer that "talks". Customer, an elderly lady, hears a voice from the computer at night. Found: a radio-clock program with the alarm set to 3 AM. Removed. The lady offered me cake.

JOB 1203 · 02/1999
Printer that prints only the letter E. Cause: a cat sleeping on the keyboard, on the E. Solution: a cushion for the cat.

JOB 1231 · 03/09/1999
New estate, terraced house. Customer: a man in his forties, office worker, two sons. Computer switches itself on at night, he says. Checked power settings, BIOS, scheduled tasks: nothing. Advised to unplug it at night. He said he does, and that in the morning the plug is back in.

JOB 1238 · 03/16/1999
Same customer. Called back. He wants the disk wiped, "everything, even the things that are not written in it". I asked him what he meant. He did not know how to say it. I started the formatting. He stood behind me all the time, looking at the screen, not at me. Halfway through he said "stop". He said it was fine, he had changed his mind, he would keep it as it was. Paid the full price. Did not want the change.

JOB 1244 · 03/24/1999
Same customer. Phone call at 3 in the morning - I do not answer at night, it was the answering machine. A whisper: "if I bring it to you, will you take it away? far away?". Then nothing. I called back in the morning. His wife answered. She said he was asleep, that he had not slept for weeks, and that she did not know anything about a phone call.

I did not go back. I should have gone back.
