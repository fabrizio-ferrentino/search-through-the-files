Your computer is ill? The doctor comes to you.

No need to unplug everything and carry the computer into town. I come to your house, I look at it where it lives, and most of the time I fix it there, in front of you, explaining what I am doing. Five years of house calls in Millbrook and the valley, more than two thousand computers cured.

What I fix: computers that do not start, that are slow, that crash; viruses; printers that print nonsense; modems that do not connect; lost files; new programs to install. What I do not fix: washing machines. People ask.

Call between 8 AM and 7 PM: I come within 24 hours, usually the same day. My number is in the phone book under "computers", and on the fridge magnet I left you last time.

Please read the notice to customers: from April I no longer take calls in the evening or at night.
