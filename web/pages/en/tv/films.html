The films of the week

The Harbour Lights drama · Wed 9 PM, Channel One
A fisherman, his three daughters and a winter in which the boats do not go out. Slow, beautiful, sad. The scene of the lights on the pier at the end is worth the whole evening.
OUR VOTE: four stars

The House at the End of the Road horror · Fri 11 PM, Network Two
A family moves into a house that the previous owners left in a hurry. The father starts to stay up at night. Nothing is shown, everything is suggested, and that is worse. Not for children, and not for adults alone in the house.
OUR VOTE: three stars and a light left on

Two Weeks in August comedy · Mon 3 PM, Channel One
Two families, one holiday house, a booking mistake. Seen a hundred times, it still works.
OUR VOTE: three stars

The Long Road West western · Mon 9 PM, Network Two
The classic of the classics. If you have never seen it, Easter Monday is the day.
OUR VOTE: five stars

Robot Friend children · Mon 5 PM, Channel One
A boy builds a robot out of a washing machine. The robot wants to go to school. Sweet, silly, perfect after lunch.
OUR VOTE: three stars (four for the under-tens)
