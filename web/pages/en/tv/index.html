Series

****
four stars
Network Two
Wednesdays 8:30 PM		 Doctor on Call
The hospital series everyone talks about at the coffee machine. Episode 14, "The night shift": a nurse alone in the ward, a patient who will not sleep and talks of his family, and a phone call at three in the morning. The season finale is a double episode on Tuesday 04/06.

**
two stars
Channel One
weekdays 1:30 PM		 The Neighbours Upstairs
The soap opera of lunchtime, now in its sixth year. This week: the wedding is cancelled, again. Our readers have bet on how many times it will be cancelled before the end of the season. The current favourite is "four".

****
four stars
Network Two
Thursdays 9 PM		 Detective in the Rain
Imported, dubbed, grey, gripping. Every case begins with a phone call in the night and ends at dawn. The detective has no family and seems to like it that way; a reader wrote to us that he finds it "the saddest thing in the series".

***
three stars
Channel One
Sundays 6 PM		 Grandpa's Farm
Three generations under one roof, a cow called Margaret and a lot of shouting at the table. Nothing happens and everybody watches it.

OUR VOTE
****
four stars
1996		 The Harbour Lights drama · Wed 9 PM, Channel One
A fisherman, his three daughters and a winter in which the boats do not go out. Slow, beautiful, sad. The scene of the lights on the pier at the end is worth the whole evening.

OUR VOTE
***
three stars and a light left on
1997		 The House at the End of the Road horror · Fri 11 PM, Network Two
A family moves into a house that the previous owners left in a hurry. The father starts to stay up at night. Nothing is shown, everything is suggested, and that is worse. Not for children, and not for adults alone in the house.

OUR VOTE
***
three stars
1994		 Two Weeks in August comedy · Mon 3 PM, Channel One
Two families, one holiday house, a booking mistake. Seen a hundred times, it still works.

Parents' corner Our advice for parents: the television is not a babysitter, but sometimes it has to be. Choose the programmes together, watch some with them, and switch it off at dinner. Dinner is for talking - even if you only talk about the space hamsters.

Viewers' letters

Gloria
03/27/1999		 Why do they always put the best films after eleven at night? Some of us work in the morning.

Dear Gloria, because the best films are not for everybody, the channels say. We say: buy a video recorder. The editors.

Walter
03/24/1999		 My wife and I argue every evening about the remote control. Can you recommend a programme that we both like?

Dear Walter, the quiz at 8:40 PM: you can argue about the answers instead. The editors.

a night owl
03/19/1999		 I am awake every night until four and there is only teleshopping. Can you tell the channels that some of us are awake?

Dear night owl, we have told them. They answered that at four in the morning the people who are awake buy knives. We are sorry. Try the radio: at that hour it talks to you. The editors.

Mrs Ida
03/12/1999		 Is it true that television makes children stupid?

Dear Mrs Ida, not more than many other things. Television with someone next to you is a different thing from television alone. The editors.
