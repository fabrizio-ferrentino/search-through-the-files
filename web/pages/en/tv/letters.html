Viewers' letters

Gloria, 03/27/1999
Why do they always put the best films after eleven at night? Some of us work in the morning.

Dear Gloria, because the best films are not for everybody, the channels say. We say: buy a video recorder. The editors.

Walter, 03/24/1999
My wife and I argue every evening about the remote control. Can you recommend a programme that we both like?

Dear Walter, the quiz at 8:40 PM: you can argue about the answers instead. The editors.

a night owl, 03/19/1999
I am awake every night until four and there is only teleshopping. Can you tell the channels that some of us are awake?

Dear night owl, we have told them. They answered that at four in the morning the people who are awake buy knives. We are sorry. Try the radio: at that hour it talks to you. The editors.

Mrs Ida, 03/12/1999
Is it true that television makes children stupid?

Dear Mrs Ida, not more than many other things. Television with someone next to you is a different thing from television alone. The editors.
