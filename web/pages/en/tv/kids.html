For children

Every morning, 7:30 AM to 8:15 AM (Channel One): cartoons before school. The favourite this year is the one with the space hamsters, which parents find unbearable and children cannot do without.

Every afternoon, 4 PM to 6 PM (Network Two): The Treehouse, two hours of cartoons, games and a presenter in a dog costume. On Fridays the children at home can phone in and play.

Saturday morning (Valley TV): Little Champions, the local football for the under-twelves, with the commentary of a retired schoolteacher who knows every child by name.

Our advice for parents: the television is not a babysitter, but sometimes it has to be. Choose the programmes together, watch some with them, and switch it off at dinner. Dinner is for talking - even if you only talk about the space hamsters.

Easter holidays: from Thursday to Tuesday, films for children every afternoon on Channel One. The list is in Films.
