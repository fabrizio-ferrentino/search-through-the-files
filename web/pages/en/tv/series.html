Series

Doctor on Call (Network Two, Wednesdays 8:30 PM). The hospital series everyone talks about at the coffee machine. Episode 14, "The night shift": a nurse alone in the ward, a patient who will not sleep and talks of his family, and a phone call at three in the morning. The season finale is a double episode on Tuesday 04/06.

The Neighbours Upstairs (Channel One, weekdays 1:30 PM). The soap opera of lunchtime, now in its sixth year. This week: the wedding is cancelled, again. Our readers have bet on how many times it will be cancelled before the end of the season. The current favourite is "four".

Detective in the Rain (Network Two, Thursdays 9 PM). Imported, dubbed, grey, gripping. Every case begins with a phone call in the night and ends at dawn. The detective has no family and seems to like it that way; a reader wrote to us that he finds it "the saddest thing in the series".

Grandpa's Farm (Channel One, Sundays 6 PM). Three generations under one roof, a cow called Margaret and a lot of shouting at the table. Nothing happens and everybody watches it.

Season dates may change. The channels decide; we find out the day before, like you.
