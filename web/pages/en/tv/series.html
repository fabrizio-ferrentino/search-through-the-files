The soap opera of lunchtime, now in its sixth year. This week: the wedding is cancelled, again. Our readers have bet on how many times it will be cancelled before the end of the season. The current favourite is "four".

Network Two
Thursdays 9 PM		Detective in the Rain
Imported, dubbed, grey, gripping. Every case begins with a phone call in the night and ends at dawn. The detective has no family and seems to like it that way; a reader wrote to us that he finds it "the saddest thing in the series".

Three generations under one roof, a cow called Margaret and a lot of shouting at the table. Nothing happens and everybody watches it.
