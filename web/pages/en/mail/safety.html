Safety: five rules
1.Your password is yours. Nobody from WebNet will ever ask for it, by phone or by e-mail. Whoever asks for it is lying.
2.Attachments. Do not open attachments you did not expect, even from a friend: a virus can send itself from your friend's computer without your friend knowing.
3.Chain letters. "Send this to ten people or something terrible will happen". Nothing will happen. Delete it.
4.Strangers. Be careful with people you meet on the Internet. Never give your address or your phone number to someone you have only known by e-mail. Teach your children the same.
5.Shared computers. At the Internet café, at work, at the library: always log out, and close the browser when you leave.
For parents. The family computer is best kept in a room where everyone passes - the living room, the kitchen - and not in a bedroom. Not to spy: just so that nobody is alone with it for hours at night. That is good advice for adults, too.
If you think someone has used your mailbox without your permission, change your password at once and call our help line.
