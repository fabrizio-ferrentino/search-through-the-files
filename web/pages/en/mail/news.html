Service news

03/29/1999
The move to the new mail system has begun. The WebMail login stays closed until the end of the work. Mail programs keep working as usual.
03/15/1999
A new anti-spam filter. Messages that arrive from known spam senders now go into a separate folder called "Suspect". Check it every now and then: sometimes a real letter ends up there.
02/22/1999
Attachments up to 2 MB. You can now send photos and documents up to 2 MB. Remember that the person receiving them pays the phone call to download them.
01/11/1999
Happy New Year from WebNet. In 1998 our users sent eleven million messages. The busiest day was Christmas Eve; the quietest, the day of the Cup final.
11/30/1998
Mailboxes not used for six months. To free space on the servers, a mailbox that has not been opened for six months receives a reminder. After twelve months it is closed, and the messages in it are deleted.
10/05/1998
WebMail is born. From today you can read your WebNet mail from any computer, with your browser.
