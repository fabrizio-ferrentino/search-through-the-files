How much is 5 MB? About two thousand ordinary messages, or twenty with a photo attached. Text takes almost nothing; pictures take a lot. A good habit: save the photos you receive on your computer, then delete the message.
The personal page. Every subscription includes free space for a personal page, reachable at our address followed by a tilde and your user name. Many of our users have one: diaries, hobbies, family photos, the club's results. Our guide to writing HTML is in the help pages of the portal.
When the space runs out you will receive a message from us. You will not lose anything until you decide what to delete. We will never delete your messages without warning you first - except for mailboxes that have not been opened for over a year.
