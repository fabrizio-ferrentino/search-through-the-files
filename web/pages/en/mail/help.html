Help

I cannot log in. Check that the CAPS LOCK key is off: passwords distinguish capital and small letters. If you have changed your password recently, use the new one. If it still does not work, call our help line.

My mailbox is full. Each mailbox has 5 MB. When it is full, the messages sent to you come back to the sender. Delete the old messages, especially the ones with attachments, and empty the Bin: deleted messages still take space until the Bin is emptied.

I receive messages from strangers offering money, diets or miracle methods. It is spam. Do not answer, not even to say "stop": answering confirms that your address is real, and you will receive twice as many. Delete them.

Can I have a second address? Yes, for the whole family: each subscription can have up to five addresses, one for each person in the house. It is a good idea: the children's mail stays with the children, and Mum's with Mum.

How long does a message take to arrive? Usually a few seconds. When the servers are very busy, up to an hour. If a message has not arrived after a day, it will not arrive: write it again.

The help line answers from Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 7 PM. The number is on the letter that came with your connection kit. Have your user name ready - never your password.
