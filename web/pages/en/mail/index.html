Help
I cannot log in. Check that the CAPS LOCK key is off: passwords distinguish capital and small letters. If you have changed your password recently, use the new one. If it still does not work, call our help line.
My mailbox is full. Each mailbox has 5 MB. When it is full, the messages sent to you come back to the sender. Delete the old messages, especially the ones with attachments, and empty the Bin: deleted messages still take space until the Bin is emptied.
I receive messages from strangers offering money, diets or miracle methods. It is spam. Do not answer, not even to say "stop": answering confirms that your address is real, and you will receive twice as many. Delete them.
Can I have a second address? Yes, for the whole family: each subscription can have up to five addresses, one for each person in the house. It is a good idea: the children's mail stays with the children, and Mum's with Mum.
How long does a message take to arrive? Usually a few seconds. When the servers are very busy, up to an hour. If a message has not arrived after a day, it will not arrive: write it again.
The help line answers from Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 7 PM. The number is on the letter that came with your connection kit. Have your user name ready - never your password.

How much is 5 MB? About two thousand ordinary messages, or twenty with a photo attached. Text takes almost nothing; pictures take a lot. A good habit: save the photos you receive on your computer, then delete the message.
The personal page. Every subscription includes free space for a personal page, reachable at our address followed by a tilde and your user name. Many of our users have one: diaries, hobbies, family photos, the club's results. Our guide to writing HTML is in the help pages of the portal.
When the space runs out you will receive a message from us. You will not lose anything until you decide what to delete. We will never delete your messages without warning you first - except for mailboxes that have not been opened for over a year.
Safety: five rules
1. Your password is yours. Nobody from WebNet will ever ask for it, by phone or by e-mail. Whoever asks for it is lying.
2. Attachments. Do not open attachments you did not expect, even from a friend: a virus can send itself from your friend's computer without your friend knowing.
3. Chain letters. "Send this to ten people or something terrible will happen". Nothing will happen. Delete it.
4. Strangers. Be careful with people you meet on the Internet. Never give your address or your phone number to someone you have only known by e-mail. Teach your children the same.
5. Shared computers. At the Internet café, at work, at the library: always log out, and close the browser when you leave.
For parents. The family computer is best kept in a room where everyone passes - the living room, the kitchen - and not in a bedroom. Not to spy: just so that nobody is alone with it for hours at night. That is good advice for adults, too.
If you think someone has used your mailbox without your permission, change your password at once and call our help line.
