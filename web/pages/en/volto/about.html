The author

I studied anatomy for four years and did not graduate. The reasons do not concern the reader. I sign as "Dr" because the four years were real and the title is a courtesy that the Internet extends to everybody.

I began measuring faces in 1995, after a conversation that is also none of the reader's business. I began with my own. I measured it every evening for a month, convinced that I was making a mistake. I was not making a mistake. My score is 4.6. I have never published it until now, and I publish it now because readers keep asking me, with a certain malice, whether I have the courage to measure myself.

I have the courage. I do not have anything else. I live alone, in a flat with a very good mirror and a very good lamp. I work in an archive, where nobody looks at anybody's face, and I find this restful.

I am often asked what I hope to achieve. Nothing. I hope that a man reading these pages will stop blaming himself for a sentence that was passed before he could speak. Some readers tell me that this has freed them. Others stop writing. I do not know which of the two groups is larger, and I have decided not to measure it.
