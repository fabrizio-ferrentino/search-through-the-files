4. Correspondence

A selection of the letters received, with my replies. I have corrected the spelling and nothing else.

"Dear doctor, I measured myself as you say and got 4.3. My wife says your site is disgusting and that she married me for my laugh. Who should I believe?" - Believe your wife: she has already chosen, and the tables only describe the ones who are still waiting. You are outside the scope of this essay. Congratulations.

"I am 19. I scored 3.8. Is there an operation?" - There are operations, abroad, expensive, and they move a measurement by a few millimetres. I have never seen one move a score by a whole point. Save the money. I am sorry. That is all I can say, and I know it is not enough.

"Your site is the cruellest thing I have ever read. My brother spent a whole summer on it. He does not go out any more. He sits with a ruler. You did this." - The ruler did not exist before me? I publish this letter because it is honest. I have thought about it more than about any other. I have not changed a single number.

"I found your site from the board. They talk about you as if you were a prophet. Are you happy?" - No. But I am precise, which lasts longer.

Letters arrive at a rate of about ten a week. I answer all of them, by post, at night. It is the part of the work I like least and do best.
