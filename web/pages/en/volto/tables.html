2.2 A warning. Readers below 4 often write to ask if there is an error. There is no error. There is a ruler. I have measured some faces ten times at the request of their owners, always with the same result, and I stopped when I realised that I was not checking the measurement. I was checking whether the man was still hoping.

2.3 Height. Height is not part of the score because it does not belong to the face. Readers have asked me to add it. I refuse, out of pity: the tables are already hard enough to read.
