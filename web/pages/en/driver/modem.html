Where are the files? To save money on the web server, all driver files are on our FTP server. Your browser cannot download them from this page: use an FTP program, connect as "anonymous" and look in the folder with the name of the category. If you do not know what an FTP program is, see the FAQ.

The modem connects but disconnects after a few minutes? Often it is the line: someone in the house has picked up the phone, or call waiting is active. Disable call waiting before connecting, or the first call you receive will throw you off the Internet.

The noise. Yes, the screeching when it connects is normal. It is two machines shaking hands. Some readers turn the speaker off; others say they miss it when it is gone.
