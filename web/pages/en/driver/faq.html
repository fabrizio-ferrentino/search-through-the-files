Frequently asked questions

What is a driver? A small program that teaches your system how to talk to a piece of hardware. Without it, the card is in the computer but the computer does not know it is there.

What is an FTP program? A program for copying files to and from a server. Your connection kit probably includes one. Open it, type the address of our server, user name "anonymous", and your e-mail address as password. Then you see our folders as if they were on your computer.

Is it legal to download drivers? Yes. Drivers are free: the manufacturers want you to use their cards. What we publish was published first by them.

The driver for my card is not here. Write to us with the exact name and we will look for it. If the manufacturer has closed down, the driver may be lost forever. Keep your floppies.

My computer is very old. Is there still hope? There is always hope. Old computers are stubborn: they keep working long after their owners have given up on them.
