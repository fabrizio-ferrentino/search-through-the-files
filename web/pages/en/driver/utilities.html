Where are the files? To save money on the web server, all driver files are on our FTP server. Your browser cannot download them from this page: use an FTP program, connect as "anonymous" and look in the folder with the name of the category. If you do not know what an FTP program is, see the FAQ.

About Shredder. When you delete a file, it is not really gone: the system only forgets where it is, and the data stays on the disk until something else is written over it. A deleted file can often be recovered weeks later. Shredder writes over it until nothing is left. Readers use it for old letters, bank statements and, one of them wrote, "things I wrote at night and do not want to find in the morning".
