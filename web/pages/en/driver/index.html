Before you install anything: write down the exact name of your card, which is printed on the card itself and not always on the box. A driver for the "VisionMax 3D" will not work on the "VisionMax 3D Plus", and the system will tell you so with a black screen.

Black screen after installing? Restart in safe mode (press F8 when the computer starts), go to the system settings and set the display back to the standard VGA adapter. Then try again with the right driver.

No sound at all? Check, in this order: that the speakers are switched on, that the volume is not at zero, that the cable is in the green socket and not the pink one, and only then the driver. Our readers report that 70% of sound problems are solved at step one.

The modem connects but disconnects after a few minutes? Often it is the line: someone in the house has picked up the phone, or call waiting is active. Disable call waiting before connecting, or the first call you receive will throw you off the Internet.

The noise. Yes, the screeching when it connects is normal. It is two machines shaking hands. Some readers turn the speaker off; others say they miss it when it is gone.

The page comes out full of strange symbols? The wrong driver is installed: the computer is talking to the printer in a language it does not understand. Remove the printer from the system and install it again with the right driver.

About Shredder. When you delete a file, it is not really gone: the system only forgets where it is, and the data stays on the disk until something else is written over it. A deleted file can often be recovered weeks later. Shredder writes over it until nothing is left. Readers use it for old letters, bank statements and, one of them wrote, "things I wrote at night and do not want to find in the morning".

Frequently asked questions

What is a driver? A small program that teaches your system how to talk to a piece of hardware. Without it, the card is in the computer but the computer does not know it is there.

What is an FTP program? A program for copying files to and from a server. Your connection kit probably includes one. Open it, type the address of our server, user name "anonymous", and your e-mail address as password. Then you see our folders as if they were on your computer.

Is it legal to download drivers? Yes. Drivers are free: the manufacturers want you to use their cards. What we publish was published first by them.

The driver for my card is not here. Write to us with the exact name and we will look for it. If the manufacturer has closed down, the driver may be lost forever. Keep your floppies.

My computer is very old. Is there still hope? There is always hope. Old computers are stubborn: they keep working long after their owners have given up on them.
