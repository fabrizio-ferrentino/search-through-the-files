Lost the driver disk?

It happens to everybody. You change the computer, you reinstall the system, you move house, and the floppy with the driver of the sound card is gone forever, together with the box. DriverZone collects the drivers of more than 2,000 video cards, sound cards, modems and printers, with notes from our readers on what works and what does not.

Most searched this week:
1. VisionMax 3D 4MB (video) · 2. SoundWave 16 (audio) · 3. FastLink 56K (modem) · 4. InkStar 300 (printer) · 5. VisionMax 2D (video)
Where are the files? To save money on the web server, all driver files are on our FTP server. Your browser cannot download them from this page: use an FTP program, connect as "anonymous" and look in the folder with the name of the category. If you do not know what an FTP program is, see the FAQ.

Before you install anything: write down the exact name of your card, which is printed on the card itself and not always on the box. A driver for the "VisionMax 3D" will not work on the "VisionMax 3D Plus", and the system will tell you so with a black screen.

Y2K corner: many readers ask whether their drivers are ready for the year 2000. Drivers do not care about the date. The BIOS does: see the utilities page for the test program.
