Where are the files? To save money on the web server, all driver files are on our FTP server. Your browser cannot download them from this page: use an FTP program, connect as "anonymous" and look in the folder with the name of the category. If you do not know what an FTP program is, see the FAQ.

No sound at all? Check, in this order: that the speakers are switched on, that the volume is not at zero, that the cable is in the green socket and not the pink one, and only then the driver. Our readers report that 70% of sound problems are solved at step one.
