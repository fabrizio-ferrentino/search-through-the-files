DISCOGRAPHY

Two records so far, both recorded in a cellar and both made worse by the cellar. We like that.

SWAN DIVE (EP, November 1998)
Six songs. Recorded at the youth centre with a borrowed four-track, mixed in one night. CD, 300 copies, second printing on its way. Price: $6 at the shows, $7 by post (stamps included).

1. Concrete Swan (2:41)
2. Last Bus to the Lake (3:12)
3. Bad Weather (2:05)
4. Iron Bridge (3:30)
5. Slow Burn (4:02)
6. Dive (1:58)

WET CEMENT (cassette, May 1997)
Our first demo. Five songs, recorded in the garage, with the neighbour's dog on track three. Sold out; a few copies are said to survive in the glove compartment of the drummer's father's car. Not for sale, not reissued.

1. Wet Cement (2:20)
2. Mill Street (2:55)
3. Dog Song (1:44)
4. Tuesday (3:05)
5. Bad Weather (first version) (2:22)

Hear a riff

Here is a MIDI file of the riff of Concrete Swan, played by a computer with no feelings. Press the little play button. It is not the band, but if you turn the volume up and squint it almost is.

Buying the record: at the shows, at the Rock Cellar (ask at the bar), or by post: send the money in an envelope with your address to the band, care of the Rock Cellar, Station Road, Millbrook. There is no online shop: we do not have a credit card and we do not have a bank manager who would give us one. The shop service on this server is suspended anyway.

Reviews: "Four boys who play as if the amplifier were about to be taken away." - the local newspaper, 12/04/1998. "Too loud, but at least they mean it." - the youth centre bulletin. "Can you play it again?" - Mr. Pelletier.
