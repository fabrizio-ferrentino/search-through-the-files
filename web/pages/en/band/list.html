MAILING LIST

Once a month we send a letter with the dates of the next shows, the news and, when we have it, a photo. It is a real letter on paper, folded in three, with a stamp: we do not have the money for e-mail and Joe's brother will not let us near the computer. Write your name and your postal address. You can leave at any time by telling us at a show.

Our promise: we use your address for the band letter and for nothing else. We do not sell it, we do not lend it, we do not know anybody who would buy it. When the letter is sent, the list lives in a shoebox in Mac's wardrobe.

How many are we? 212 names on 02/27/1999: 160 in Millbrook, 31 around the lake, 14 from further away, and 7 who wrote "a fan" and no address, whom we love but cannot write to.

Last letter (03/01/1999): "Dear friends. February was a good month. We sold the last EP of the first printing, Joe's pedal was repaired, and the Rock Cellar gave us a Saturday in May. Thank you to everybody who came on the 20th of February, particularly the lady who asked for the volume to be raised. We will start the new songs in April. Please come on the 10th. Mac sends greetings and says he knows the tune."
