TOUR DATES

Everything we have played, and everything we will play. We are a local band: our "tour" is the lake and the three towns around it, and we get home by midnight because the bass player's mother worries. If you want to book us, see the guestbook.

Rider (for those who ask): a plug, a stage, a bucket for the drummer's towels, and water. We bring the rest in the back of somebody's mother's car.

Setlist at the last show (Rock Cellar, 03/13/1999): Concrete Swan, Wet Cement, Last Bus to the Lake, Bad Weather, Iron Bridge, Slow Burn, Mill Street, Dive. About 35 minutes, plus one encore if they ask nicely.

Directions: the Rock Cellar is behind the railway station: go down the steps by the newsagent. The Anchor Inn is on Lake Road, opposite the boat hire. Parking is easy except on market day.

Thanks to Mr. Pelletier, the Rock Cellar, the Anchor, the youth centre, the cousin with the guitars, and everyone who ever stood in the front row and did not leave.
