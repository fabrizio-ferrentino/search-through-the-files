LYRICS

Words by Chris, most of them written on the bus. Do not use them without asking; do ask, we like it.

CONCRETE SWAN
(from Swan Dive)

The lake is grey and the sky is lower
than the roof of the bus shelter,
and I'm standing here in my father's coat
waiting for something to change.

Concrete swan, concrete swan,
you never learned to fly,
you sit on the edge of the fountain
and watch the cars go by.

They say you stay where you were put,
they say that's how it's done,
but I heard you whisper, late last night,
that you were made to run.

LAST BUS TO THE LAKE
(from Swan Dive)

Eleven forty on Station Road,
the driver counts the change,
the girl in the corner seat is asleep
and the window shows my face.

Last bus, last bus to the lake,
take me home the slow way round,
past the bridge and the boat hire sheds,
past the lights of the sleeping town.

BAD WEATHER
(from Swan Dive)

It rained on the day we met,
it rained on the day you left,
I'm not blaming the weather,
the weather is all I've got.

Bad weather, bad weather,
come in and sit by the fire,
you can't be worse than the people
who tell me what I should desire.
