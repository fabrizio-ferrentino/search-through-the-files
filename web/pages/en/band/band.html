THE BAND

Four people, one van that is not ours, and a rehearsal room that smells of wet carpet. We met at Millbrook High and in the youth centre; three of us are still at school, and one has a job at the hardware store.

CHRIS HALE - voice, guitar (age 18)
Writes the words, usually on the bus, and the tunes, usually in the bath. Wants to be a poet but is afraid of the poets. Favourite food: chips. Favourite band: not telling.

MAC PELLINI - lead guitar (age 19)
Learned from a cousin and a video tape. Can play the solo of any song after hearing it twice, and cannot remember his own phone number. Plays a guitar with a repaired neck. Favourite food: pizza with everything.

TOMMY BRIGHTWELL - bass (age 17)
The calm one. Says almost nothing between songs and then talks to the whole audience. Carries the gear because "somebody has to". Lives with a mother who brings tea to rehearsals.

JOE KESSLER - drums (age 20)
The oldest, with a part-time job and a drum kit he is still paying for. Hits hard, breaks sticks, keeps a box of them as trophies. His brother has the only computer in this story.

Influences: everything our parents did not want us to hear, plus a lot of what they did. We will not name bands, because the list changes every week.

Where we rehearse: in Joe's garage on Mill Street, Tuesday and Friday evenings from seven to ten, and Sunday afternoons if the neighbours are out. The neighbour on the left has asked us to stop at nine. We stop at ten. Only once did the police come, and they stayed for two songs.

The photo: not on this page yet. The newspaper photographer took it on the iron bridge on 03/14/1999; we will scan it when the youth centre scanner is free. In it Tommy is laughing and Mac is looking at the wrong camera.
