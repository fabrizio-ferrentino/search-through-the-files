LOUD. FAST. FROM MILLBROOK.

Welcome to the official site of Concrete Swans, four boys from the lake who practise in a garage on Mill Street while the neighbours bang on the wall. We play guitar music with no keyboards, no violins and no apologies. This is where you find out where we play, where to buy the record, what the words are and how to get on the mailing list so you never miss a show.

We started in the spring of 1997 with one amp, two guitars borrowed from a cousin and a drum kit with a broken pedal. Now we have two amps, our own guitars and a drum kit with a better pedal. Our first record, the cassette Wet Cement, sold out. The EP Swan Dive came out in November 1998 and we are still carrying the boxes up the stairs.

Listen: the music page has a little MIDI demo of one of our riffs. It does not sound like us at all, but it shows the tune. The real thing is on the EP. If you are in the area you can also hear us on the Friday night show of the local radio station, which plays the EP at 11:00 PM when the presenter is in a good mood.

Contact: there is no e-mail address because our drummer's brother owns the only computer and keeps it locked. To book us for a party, a dance or a festival, sign the guestbook or leave a message at the Rock Cellar, to the attention of the band. We play free for schools and for the hospital fete.
