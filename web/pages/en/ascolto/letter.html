A letter from a volunteer

I am one of the six who were left. In September I came back to the office alone, one evening, to read the board. I had not been able to for weeks. I found the baker's messages.

I read them in order. The first one said it was not serious. The last one said never mind. Between the two there were two months, and nobody.

I wrote back. I wrote: how are you? I wrote it under every one of his messages, because he had asked for it, and because it was the only thing he had asked for. The mails came back. The address no longer existed.

I do not know who he was. I do not know what happened to him. I know that he asked a very small thing, and that a very small thing was not there.

If you are reading this and you are alone: write to someone tonight. Not to us, we are not there. To a brother, an old friend, a neighbour. Ask them how they are. And if someone asks you, tell them the truth.

- Anna, volunteer, 09/1998
