Volunteers wanted

To start again we need at least twenty volunteers: two per night, every night, plus a few for the message board. We had thirty-two in 1997. By the summer of 1998 we were six.

Why did they leave? For the best reasons: a new job, a baby, a move to another city. And for the hardest ones: listening, night after night, is tiring, and nobody listens to the listeners.

Who can become a volunteer: anyone over twenty-one who can stay awake, keep a secret and stay quiet when the other person needs silence. We give a free training course of six evenings. You do not need to be a psychologist. You need to be a person.

How to apply: write to the association through the WebNet directory, or leave a note at the parish office in the main square. We read every letter.

Applications received since 07/1998: 3.
