You are not the only one awake

The service is suspended. Since the summer we no longer have enough volunteers to cover the nights or to answer the message board. We hope to start again soon. If you need help now, talk to your doctor, or call the emergency services.

Someone Listens was born in 1995 from an idea of five friends: a telephone line, open at night, for people who are alone and need to talk. Not to be cured, not to be advised. Just to talk to someone who listens.

In 1997 we added this site, with a message board: you could leave a message at any hour, and a volunteer would answer it within a few days. Many of the people who wrote to us did not want to use the phone. Writing is easier, they said. You do not have to hear your own voice.

We received thousands of messages. We answered most of them. Not all. This site stays online as it was, because some of the people who wrote to us might come back to see if anyone answered.
