The message board

Some of the messages and answers of the last months, published with the consent of those who wrote them.

message 4107 · 02/1998
"I am seventy and my wife died in the autumn. The children call on Sundays. The other six days are very long." - Ernest

Dear Ernest, six days are long, yes. Do you know that the library in your town has a reading group on Thursdays? Go once. Then write and tell us. - Anna, volunteer
message 4122 · 03/1998
"I am nineteen, everyone at university seems to have friends already. I eat lunch alone every day." - L.

Dear L., so do many of the people at the other tables. Sit next to one of them tomorrow. Say "is this seat free?". It is the hardest sentence in the world and it takes two seconds. - Marco, volunteer
message 4150 · 04/1998
"I just wanted to say thank you. I called you in January. I am better now." - a woman from the coast

Thank you for coming back to tell us. It is the most beautiful kind of message. - all of us
