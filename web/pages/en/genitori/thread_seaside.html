dad_of_two · posted Friday, 02/12/1999 at 10:10 PM
Hi everyone, first post! In two weeks I'm taking my wife and the boys to the seaside, two weeks in a guesthouse, the first real holiday since the little one was born. The big one is at the age where he wants to do everything himself, the little one at the age where he wants to do everything the big one does. You experienced dads: what do I pack? What do I NOT pack? How do you survive two boys on a beach?

dave_dad · posted Friday, 02/12/1999 at 10:31 PM
Welcome! Rule one: YOU put the sun cream on, not them. Rule two: buckets and spades in PAIRS, identical, same colour. Trust me. Rule three: ice cream is only ever promised for afterwards, never before.

rob_the_dad · posted Friday, 02/12/1999 at 11:05 PM
I'll add: one ball, just one, and a marble run dug in the sand. Keeps them busy for hours and you get to read half a page of the newspaper. Half. Don't push it.

mum_louise · posted Saturday, 02/13/1999 at 8:40 AM
From a mum: bring one more dry change than you think, one more hat (they always lose one) and a bit more patience. And take a photo of all four of you together, because in the albums there are always only the kids and never the parents!

dad_of_two · posted, three weeks later, Sunday, 03/07/1999 at 9:15 PM
We're back! An update for those who helped: buckets in pairs, you were right. The little one built his first sandcastle, it fell down after ten seconds and he cried for half an hour. The big one built it again for him, bigger, with a moat. I pretended to read the paper and watched them instead.

My wife says that on holiday I was the third child. She's right. We took the photo of all four of us, mum_louise: we're having it printed big for the living room. Thanks everyone, this forum is a good place.
