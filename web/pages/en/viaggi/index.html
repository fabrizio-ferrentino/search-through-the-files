Family holidays

Travelling with children is a different kind of travel: shorter journeys, earlier dinners, and a swimming pool counts more than a museum. Our family holidays are chosen with this in mind, by people who have children themselves.

  • Hotels with a children's club, so the parents can read a book by the pool for an hour.
  • Rooms with a cot and a bottle warmer on request.
  • Beaches with shallow water and a lifeguard.
  • Babysitting in the evening in the best hotels, with staff we know.
  • Children under twelve free in their parents' room if you book by 04/30/1999.

The agency

Sun & Sea opened in 1979 in a single room above the chemist's, with one desk, one telephone and a map of the world on the wall. Today we have four desks and two telephones, and the map is still there, with a pin in every place a customer has sent us a postcard from. There are more than six hundred.

Who we are
Mrs Carla, who founded the agency and still knows every hotel on the coast by name; her daughter Elena, who handles the long journeys; Mark, who is twenty-four and is the one who made this site; and Fiore, the cat, who sleeps on the catalogues.		 Since
