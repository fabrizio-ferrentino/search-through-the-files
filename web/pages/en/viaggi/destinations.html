Our destinations

The sea at home. Our coast has everything: long sandy beaches for families, rocky coves for those who like to swim far, and the small fishing villages where the evening smells of grilled fish. We work with forty family-run hotels that we have visited one by one.

The islands. Clear water, white houses and slow days. In June and September they are at their best: warm sea, few people, and prices a third lower than in August.

The mountains. Walks in summer, skiing in winter, and a silence that city people do not believe until they hear it. For families we recommend the chalets of the eastern valleys, with a garden and a stove.

The cities of art. Museums, churches, squares and the best ice cream of your life. Our tip: book the museum tickets with us, and you skip the queue.

Abroad. The capitals of Europe by train or plane, and for the brave, the long journeys: the deserts, the safaris, the great cities across the ocean. For those, come and talk to us in person: a long journey is planned at a table, not on a screen.
