Holidays with children

Travelling with children is a different kind of travel: shorter journeys, earlier dinners, and a swimming pool counts more than a museum. Our family holidays are chosen with this in mind, by people who have children themselves.

  • Hotels with a children's club, so the parents can read a book by the pool for an hour.
  • Rooms with a cot and a bottle warmer on request.
  • Beaches with shallow water and a lifeguard.
  • Babysitting in the evening in the best hotels, with staff we know.
  • Children under twelve free in their parents' room if you book by 04/30/1999.
