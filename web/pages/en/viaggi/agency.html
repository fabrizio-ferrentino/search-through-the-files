The agency

Sun & Sea opened in 1979 in a single room above the chemist's, with one desk, one telephone and a map of the world on the wall. Today we have four desks and two telephones, and the map is still there, with a pin in every place a customer has sent us a postcard from. There are more than six hundred.

Who we are: Mrs Carla, who founded the agency and still knows every hotel on the coast by name; her daughter Elena, who handles the long journeys; Mark, who is twenty-four and is the one who made this site; and Fiore, the cat, who sleeps on the catalogues.

Opening hours: Monday to Friday 9 AM to 12:30 PM and 3:30 PM to 7:30 PM, Saturday morning. In summer we also open on Saturday afternoon, because that is when people decide where to go.

Send us a postcard from your holiday. We will add a pin to the map.
