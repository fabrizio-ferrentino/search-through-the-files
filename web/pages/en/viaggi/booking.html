How to book

Online booking is not available. We tried it for two months and too many people booked the wrong dates. A holiday is booked with a person: come to the agency or phone us.

At the agency. Bring an identity document for everyone who is travelling, and a rough idea of how much you want to spend. We will show you what is available and hold it for you for three days while you decide.

The deposit. To confirm, you pay 25% of the price. The rest one month before departure.

Insurance. We always recommend the cancellation insurance: it costs little, and if someone gets ill before leaving, you get your money back. We have seen too many families lose a holiday for a fever.

Documents. For travel abroad, check that passports are valid for at least six months after your return. Children need their own document. We will tell you which ones, depending on the country.
