The rooms

Main Square
The big room. Everybody passes through, everybody talks over everybody. Not for the shy.
Twenty-Somethings
For people between twenty and thirty. Music, holidays, exams, parties.
Over Forty
Calmer. Children, work, recipes, and the occasional serious conversation.
Sport Bar
Football, mostly. On Sunday evening, only football.
Night Owls
From one in the morning. The people who cannot sleep, work nights, or do not want to go to bed. Fewer people, longer conversations.
Hearts
For people looking for someone. Be honest, be kind, and do not send the same message to everyone.

Each room holds up to fifty people. When it is full, you wait at the door. When it is empty, you can wait inside.
