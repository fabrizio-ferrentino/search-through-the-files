The rules

  1. Be kind. Behind every nickname there is a person, maybe having a bad day.
  2. No insults, no threats, no flooding (writing the same thing many times).
  3. Never give your address or phone number in a room. Use private messages, and only with people you trust.
  4. Do not WRITE IN CAPITALS: it means you are shouting.
  5. Say hello when you come in and goodbye when you leave. It is good manners, online too.
  6. If someone is being bothered, tell a moderator. If someone seems to be in real trouble, tell a moderator too.

Moderators have a star next to their nickname. They are volunteers, not police. They can warn you, mute you or, in serious cases, close your nickname.

Every room repeats the main rules automatically every half hour. Yes, that message that nobody reads. Read it once.
