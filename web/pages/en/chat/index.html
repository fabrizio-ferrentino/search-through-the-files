Welcome to ChatPoint

The chat server is offline. We are moving ChatPoint to a new machine that can hold more people at once. During the move the rooms are closed. The pages of the site, the rules and the archive stay online.

ChatPoint is the place to talk live with people from all over the country, and from abroad too. Choose a nickname, enter a room, and say hello: someone will answer. Usually.

On a normal evening ChatPoint has between two and three hundred people connected, most of them between nine and midnight. The busiest room is Main Square; the quietest, Night Owls, which wakes up after one in the morning.

New to chat? Read the rules and learn the smileys. And remember: you do not have to be funny to chat. You just have to be there.
