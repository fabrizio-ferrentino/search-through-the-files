Smileys: the faces of the chat

:-)a smile
;-)a wink: I am joking
:-(sad
:-Da big laugh
:-Osurprise
:-Psticking your tongue out
:'-(crying
:-|no expression. I am here. I do not know what to say.

Tilt your head to the left to see them. In a written conversation nobody sees your face, so the smileys do the work of the face: they say whether you are joking, whether you are hurt, whether you are smiling. Use them. A sentence without a face can sound much colder than you meant it.

Abbreviations: LOL = laughing out loud; BRB = be right back; ASL = age, sex, location (the first question in every room, and not a polite one); GN = good night.
